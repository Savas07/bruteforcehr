Settings › Data & privacy: "Export everything" builds a ZIP of all your data (readable JSON per collection, your CV and attachment files, a README; never passwords or tokens), downloadable for 7 days. "Delete account" removes every document, file and the sign-in account, revoking mail access at Google first, after you confirm by typing a word and, if needed, signing in again with an email link. On the Mac/PC and iPhone.
