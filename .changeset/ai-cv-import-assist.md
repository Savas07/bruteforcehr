Import a CV from PDF, Word, JSON Resume or a LinkedIn data export. Rewrite, shorten, strengthen or translate CV text (English, Turkish, German) with AI. The AI never adds facts that aren't in your CV: unknown numbers become placeholders like `[X%]` for you to fill in. AI features can be turned off in your profile, and each account has a daily AI limit.
