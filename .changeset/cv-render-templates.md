Three CV templates (Classic, Modern, Compact) that applicant tracking systems read correctly, with accent colours, font pairs, density and A4/Letter. Section headings and dates follow the CV's language (English, Turkish, German). Export to PDF, Word and JSON Resume, named `First-Last-CV-Company.pdf`. German and Turkish CVs can add a photo and date of birth; both are off unless you turn them on.
