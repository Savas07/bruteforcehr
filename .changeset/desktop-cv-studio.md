CV Studio works on your real CVs: create one (empty or from your profile), import a PDF, Word file, JSON Resume or LinkedIn export and review what was read, edit with a live preview that is exactly the exported PDF, pick a template, accent and fonts, and export PDF (made on this computer), Word or JSON with a `First-Last-CV-Company.pdf` name. Each editing session saves one version; version history compares and restores, and edits made on another device at the same time ask which version to keep. The ATS check reads your exported PDF back and compares it with a pasted job description, and "Improve with AI" suggests rewrites you accept one by one (placeholders like [X%] must be filled first).
