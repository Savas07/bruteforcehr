Sign in with your email, no password: enter your address, then open the link we email you on this computer. It takes you back to the app and signs you in. If you asked for the link on another device, the app asks for your address once. Expired, used or broken links get a clear message and a way to send a new one. Gmail, Outlook and other webmail addresses get an "Open …" button. "Sign Out" is in the app menu (macOS) or File (Windows) and in the command palette.
