First-run setup on the desktop. After you sign in for the first time, three short steps get you started: add your CV (import a file or start an empty one), tell us what you're looking for (roles, seniority, remote / hybrid / on-site, places with "I'd move here", a private salary floor with currency and per month or per year, whether you can work in each country without visa sponsorship, and your languages), then start your first saved search, which runs right away and lands you on Today. Every step can be skipped, and setup picks up where you left off if you close the app. The same preferences live in the new Settings › Job preferences, which saves as you go.
