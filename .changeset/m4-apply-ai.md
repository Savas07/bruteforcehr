Apply with AI help: "Tailor for this job" proposes edits to your CV that you accept or keep one by one, saved as a new version; a cover letter draft from your CV and the job in a formal, warm or concise tone, with your own reusable templates; and an answers library (work authorization, notice period, salary expectation, links, common questions) ready to copy into company application forms. The AI only rewords, reorders and emphasises what your CV already says: an edit or sentence that would add an employer, number, date or skill you don't have is removed.
