Apply on the desktop, start to finish: "Tailor for this job" (from the Apply panel or CV Studio's Check tab) shows each suggested edit with the original, the suggestion, why and what in your CV it is based on, a live preview and Accept / Keep original per edit or all at once, and saves the result as a version for that application without changing your CV. Cover letters are drafted with AI in a formal, warm or concise tone and your language, or started from a starter or your own template, with fields such as the hiring manager filled before sending. After opening a company's site, the application kit has your CV as a file (open, show in Finder / Explorer, copy its path), your cover letter (copy, save as PDF) and your saved answers, each with Copy. Settings › Application answers manages those answers per language, country and currency, and emails can carry more files from your saved PDFs or your computer, within your account's size limit.
