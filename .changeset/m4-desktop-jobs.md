Jobs on your real data: a feed ranked by match with filters (work mode, location, seniority, salary, posted, match, visa, source), sort, keyboard triage (J/K, S save, D Not for me with optional reasons), live search of the job sites, saved searches with alerts, adding a job from a link, and applying — on the company site with "Did you apply?", or by email from the app (or your mail app until an account can send).
