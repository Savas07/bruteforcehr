iPhone: the Jobs tab is live. Matches from your job sources with their band, reasons and gaps; search across job sites; filters for work mode, match, date, salary, location, seniority and source; save (straight into the Tracker) or hide with an optional reason, both with Undo; saved searches with alerts and a Hidden jobs list; add a job from a link. Apply from any job: pick the CV and cover letter, then open the company site in Safari and answer "Did you apply?", or email it with your CV and cover letter as PDFs (from a connected account with 10 seconds to undo, or from Mail). The application keeps the exact CV version you sent. Push notifications are wired up for alerts and interview mail once the APNs key is in place.
