## Two ways to apply

Open a job and choose **Apply**. The button follows the posting:

- **Apply on company site** when the posting links to an application form.
- **Apply by email** when the posting gives an address. You can also choose **Apply by email instead**, or, on iPhone, enter a recruiter's address yourself.

BruteForceHR never fills in or sends anything without you. On a company site you fill in their form yourself; by email, nothing is sent until you've reviewed the message and pressed **Send**.

## Apply on the company site

1. Choose **Apply on company site**. The posting's application page opens in your browser (on iPhone, in Safari inside the app).
2. Fill in their form. On iPhone, the Apply sheet keeps your CV and cover letter as PDFs: use **Share › Save to Files** so the form's file picker can find them.
3. Come back to BruteForceHR. It asks **Did you submit your application?**
   - **Yes, I applied** moves the application to **Applied** in your Tracker.
   - **Not yet** asks again next time you open the job.
   - **I'm not applying** keeps the job in Saved.

## Apply by email

1. Choose **Apply by email**. The composer opens beside the job (on iPhone, as a sheet).
2. Check the fields:
   - **From**: one of your [connected email accounts](/guide/mail/).
   - **To**: the address from the job posting.
   - **Subject** and **Message**: started for you in the language of the posting. Edit them as you like.
   - **CV**: the CV to attach. Its current version goes as a PDF and is kept with the application.
   - **Cover letter**: turn on **Attach a cover letter** to write one. It's attached as a PDF in your CV's design.
3. Choose **Review email**, read it through, then choose **Send to …** (the button names the address).
4. The email waits 10 seconds before it goes, with **Undo** in case you change your mind. **Send now** skips the wait.

When it's sent, the application moves to **Applied**, and the Tracker keeps the exact files you sent. The email is in your account's Sent folder like any other.

<div class="guide-note">

**Sending permission.** For a Gmail or Outlook account connected with Google or Microsoft sign-in, BruteForceHR asks for permission to send the first time you apply from it. Accounts connected with an app password need their outgoing (SMTP) server set; if they're connected for reading only, use **Reconnect** in **Settings › Mail accounts** to add it.

</div>

## Without a connected email account

You can still apply by email from your own mail app:

1. In the composer, **From** shows **Your mail app**. Save the CV and cover letter as PDFs.
2. Choose **Open in your mail app** (on iPhone, **Open in Mail**), attach the PDFs and send it from there.
3. Back in BruteForceHR, choose **I sent it**, so your Tracker records the application with the files you used.

## Limits and errors

- Sending needs an internet connection. Offline, your draft is saved and waits for you.
- There's a daily sending limit. If you reach it, the draft is saved and you can send it tomorrow.
- If the attachments are too large for your provider, try a shorter CV or leave out the cover letter.
- If an account needs reconnecting, BruteForceHR says so and keeps your draft. See [Troubleshooting](/guide/troubleshooting/#sending-an-application).
