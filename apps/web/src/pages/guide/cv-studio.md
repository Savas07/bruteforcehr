## Your CVs

**CVs** lists your CVs, each with its language and how many applications it was sent with. One is your **Default**: matching and Apply use it unless you pick another. From a CV's **⋯** menu you can rename, duplicate, archive it or open its version history. Archived CVs keep their history and wait under **Archived**.

On Mac and Windows the screen has three parts: the list, the editor, and a live preview of the page. On iPhone, open a CV and switch between **Edit** and **Preview**.

## Import the CV you have

1. Choose **New CV**, then **Import a CV…**.
2. Pick or drop a PDF, a Word document (DOCX), a JSON Resume file, or the ZIP from LinkedIn's "Get a copy of your data".
3. BruteForceHR reads the file and asks **Did we read your CV correctly?** Fields it wasn't sure about are marked, with the reason. Fix them, or choose **Looks right**. **Show only fields to check** hides everything else.
4. Give the CV a name and choose **Create CV**.

Your original file stays attached to the new CV. Reading a PDF or Word file uses AI when AI features are on in [Settings](/guide/settings/#ai); check names, numbers and dates before you create the CV.

## Start from scratch

Choose **New CV**, then **Start a new CV…**. Start with an **Empty page** or **From your profile**, which fills in your name, contact details, languages and skills. Pick the CV's language: headings, dates and spell-check follow it.

## Edit

- Each section (header, summary, experience, projects, skills, education, languages and more) opens and closes on its own. Move an entry or a section with the arrows, or with <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> (<kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> on Windows).
- Your changes save as you type, also offline. The preview shows the page exactly as it will be exported.
- If you edit the same CV on two devices while one is offline, BruteForceHR asks which version to keep. **Compare** shows the lines that differ, and the other version stays in the version history either way.

## Versions for other roles and languages

A **variant** is a copy of a CV for another role or another language. It stays linked to its base CV, so when you change the base, the variant tells you where ("The base changed in 2 places") and lets you review the changes.

1. From a CV's **⋯** menu, choose **New variant…**.
2. Choose **Another role** or **Another language**.
3. For a language variant, the text is copied as it is; translate it in the editor, where **Translate** suggestions can help.

## Suggestions

Suggestions help you word what's already true. They never add employers, job titles, dates, degrees, skills or numbers you didn't write.

- Select a line and choose **Improve with AI** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd>): **Rewrite**, **Shorten**, **Stronger verb**, **Add a result** or **Translate**.
- Each suggestion shows what changes and why. Choose **Use suggestion** or **Keep original**; nothing changes in your CV until you do.
- **Add a result** leaves a placeholder for the number only you know. Fill it in before you export; the export reminds you.

Suggestions are made by Anthropic's Claude on BruteForceHR's server, and only when AI features are on in [Settings › AI](/guide/settings/#ai).

## Check a CV against a job

The **Check** panel compares your CV with a saved job's keywords: what's **Covered** and what's **Missing**. For a missing skill you really have, choose **Add to Skills**; otherwise **I don't have this**. Keyword checks are a guide, not a guarantee.

## Design

In **Design**, choose the template, accent colour, fonts, spacing and paper size (A4 or US Letter). Every template is one column with standard headings and real text, so application systems can read every section.

German and Turkish employers may expect a photo and date of birth; UK and US employers usually don't. Both are off by default and can be turned on per CV under **Photo and date of birth**.

## Version history

BruteForceHR keeps the earlier versions of every CV. **Version history** lists them with what changed and which ones were **Sent with applications**. **Compare with now** shows the differences, and **Restore this version** brings an older one back; your current version stays in the history, so you can switch back anytime.

## Export

Choose **Export** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>E</kbd>), then the format: **PDF**, **Word (DOCX)** or **JSON Resume**, and the paper size. Add the company's name to put it in the file name. On Mac and Windows the file is made on your computer and nothing is uploaded. On iPhone, **Share PDF** in the preview opens the share sheet.
