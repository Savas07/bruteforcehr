## What BruteForceHR stores

Everything is stored under your account with Google Cloud in Belgium, and only your account can read it. It's never sold or used for ads.

- Your profile, job preferences and settings.
- Your CVs with every version, cover letters, and the files you imported or created.
- Jobs, matches, saved searches and the companies you follow.
- Applications with their history, interviews, contacts, notes and reminders.
- Job-related emails from your connected inboxes. Emails that aren't about your job search are never stored.
- Passwords and sign-in tokens for your mail accounts, encrypted with a key only BruteForceHR's servers hold. No app can read them, not even yours.

The apps also keep a copy on each device, so they work offline. Protect your devices with a passcode or password.

The [Privacy Policy](/privacy/) has the details, including who processes what.

## How long it's kept

- Most data stays until you delete it, or delete your account.
- The text of a job-related email is kept while its application is active, then deleted 180 days after the application closes. Emails not linked to an application lose their text 180 days after they arrived.
- Disconnecting an email account deletes the job emails stored from it.
- An export file is deleted from the server 7 days after it's created.

## AI and your data

AI features run on BruteForceHR's server with Anthropic's Claude: reading imported CVs, suggesting CV wording, explaining job matches, and, only if you allow it, sorting job emails. Only the text a task needs is sent, never your whole mailbox or account, and Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models.

Turn either switch off in **Settings › AI**. See [Settings](/guide/settings/#ai).

## Export everything

1. Open **Settings › Data & privacy** and choose **Create export**.
2. Preparing usually takes under a minute.
3. When it says **Export ready**, choose **Save…** (on iPhone, **Save to Files** or **Share**).

You get a ZIP file with readable JSON files of everything listed above, plus your CV files. It doesn't include the passwords and tokens for your mail accounts, or emails that weren't about your job search, because those were never stored. You can make up to 5 exports a day; a new export replaces the previous one.

## Delete your account

Deleting your account permanently removes everything: your profile, CVs with every version, applications and their history, saved jobs and searches, stored emails, connected mail accounts (BruteForceHR also removes its access at Google, Microsoft or your mail provider) and your devices and notification settings. It can't be undone, and there are no backups to restore from.

1. Open **Settings › Data & privacy** and choose **Delete account…**. To keep a copy, choose **Export my data first**.
2. For your security, BruteForceHR emails you a sign-in link. Open it on the same device to confirm it's you.
3. Type **DELETE** and choose **Delete everything**.

If deletion stops halfway, for example because the connection dropped, choose **Try again**: it continues where it stopped. For accounts connected with an app password, also delete the app password at your provider.

## Your rights

You can see, download, correct and delete everything yourself in the app. Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK, you also have further rights; the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/#your-choices-and-rights) explains them and how to reach us.
