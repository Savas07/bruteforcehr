## Open Insights

On Mac and Windows, choose **Insights** in the sidebar. On iPhone, open it from the Tracker, or from the link at the bottom of Today.

Insights is a read-only view of your Tracker. It starts once you've moved an application to **Applied**, and it counts only what you've recorded or confirmed.

## Key numbers

- **Applications**: how many you sent in the chosen range.
- **Response rate**, **Interview rate**, **Offer rate**: how many of those got a reply, reached an interview or an offer. A rate appears once at least 5 applications count towards it, so a single early reply doesn't read as a trend.
- **First response**: the median number of days until the first reply, and how many are still waiting.

Choose the **ⓘ** next to a number to see exactly how it's counted.

## How far your applications get

The funnel shows how many applications reached each step, **Applied**, **Response**, **Interview** and **Offer**, with the share that moved on from the step before and the median time it took. The table under it has the same numbers for screen readers and for copying.

## Where responses come from

**Where do responses come from?** compares response rates by a dimension of your choice under **Group by**: the job's source, how you applied (the channel), the CV variant you sent, or the market. Groups with fewer than 5 applications are listed last, without a rate.

**How active have I been?** shows the last 12 weeks: applications sent, follow-ups and interviews.

## Ranges, filters and comparing

- Pick the range at the top: on Mac and Windows from 30 days to all time, or a custom range; on iPhone 4 weeks, 12 weeks or all time.
- Filter by **Source**, **Channel**, **CV variant** or **Market**.
- **Compare to previous period** shows how each number changed.

## From a number to the applications behind it

Every number is a link. Select one, for example the 3 offers in the funnel, to open the Tracker filtered to exactly those applications.

## Export

On Mac and Windows, **Export… › CSV · this view** saves the numbers you're looking at as a spreadsheet file.
