## Where jobs come from

Jobs brings openings together from three places:

- **Job sites.** Public job boards and job APIs for Turkey, Germany and the rest of the EU, the UK, the US and remote work. Some of them need an access key that the app's owner sets up; until then they're listed as not in use. The [Features page](/features/#sources) lists every source.
- **Companies you follow.** Add a company's careers page in **Settings › Companies you follow** and its new openings appear in Jobs without searching. Pages hosted on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Recruitee and Personio work, and so do careers pages that list their jobs.
- **Job alert emails.** When your [connected inbox](/guide/mail/) receives job alerts from job sites, their postings go to Jobs too.

The same job found on several sites is shown once, with every source listed under **Found on**. **Settings › Job sources** shows whether each source is working and when it last found jobs.

## Search and filter

- Type in **Search** to filter the jobs you already have by title, company, place or skill.
- To look for new jobs, type a role or skill and choose **Search job sites**. New jobs show up in the list as they're found.
- Narrow the list with the filters: **Work mode**, **Location**, **Seniority**, **Salary**, **Posted**, **Match**, **Visa sponsorship** and **Source**. On iPhone, they're under **Filters**.
- The lists at the top (on iPhone, the chips) switch between **All jobs**, **Saved**, each saved search and, on Mac and Windows, **Hidden jobs**.

## How a job fits you

Every job shows how well it fits, from **Stretch** through **Partial** and **Good** to **Strong**, with a score out of 100. Open a job to see why:

- Each requirement (place, work mode, seniority, salary, visa sponsorship, language) is checked against your [job preferences](/guide/settings/#job-preferences), with a quote from the posting it came from.
- Skills the posting asks for are split into **In your CV** and **Not in your CV yet**.
- The match is based on one of your CVs. Choose **Change** to match against another one.

## Save, hide or open a job

- **Save** keeps a job in **Saved** and adds it to your [Tracker](/guide/tracker/) in the Saved stage.
- **Not for me** hides a job from your list. Give a reason if you like (salary, location, seniority and so on); reasons tune your matches. Hidden jobs stay readable under **Hidden jobs**, where **Show in my feed again** brings one back.
- From the **⋯** menu you can hide every job from a company, or copy the link to the posting.
- **Open original** opens the posting on the site it came from.

## Saved searches

A saved search checks the job sites for you on a schedule and shows new matches under Jobs, in the sidebar with a count of new ones.

1. Search and set the filters you want.
2. Choose **Save search** and give it a name.
3. Pick how often to check and when to tell you:
   - **As they arrive**: checked every hour, with an alert for each new match.
   - **Daily**: checked once a day, with an alert for each new match. **Also send a daily digest** adds one summary a day.
   - **Weekly**: checked once a week.
   - **Off**: the search stays in your list, with no checks and no alerts.
4. Under **Notify me for**, choose all new matches or only those above a score.

Manage them in **Saved searches…**: rename, change what to search for (use OR for alternatives, like "frontend OR product engineer"), **Run now**, **Show results** or **Delete**, which you can undo.

## Add from a link

Found a job somewhere else? Add it from its link and BruteForceHR reads the posting for you.

1. In Jobs, choose **Add from link** (on iPhone, **Add a job from a link**).
2. Paste the link and choose **Read posting**. Company career pages, job boards and LinkedIn postings all work.
3. Check the details. Fields we weren't sure about are marked **Check this**, and nothing is saved until you choose **Save job**.

The job goes to **Saved**. If a site doesn't let BruteForceHR read its pages, or the posting is gone, choose **Enter it yourself**. Reading a page needs an internet connection, and there's a daily limit on how many links you can read.

## Save from the share sheet on iPhone

In Safari or any app that shares a job's link:

1. Tap **Share** and choose **BruteForceHR**. (If you don't see it, scroll the row of apps and tap **More**.)
2. BruteForceHR reads the posting. Check the role, company and location.
3. Tap **Save** to keep it in Jobs › Saved, or **Save and log as applied** if you've already applied: it's added to your Tracker as Applied today.

If the page can't be read, tap **Add it by hand**; the link is kept in the notes. You need to be signed in to the BruteForceHR app first.
