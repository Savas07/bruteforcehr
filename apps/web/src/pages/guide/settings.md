## Open Settings

- **Mac and Windows:** choose **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd> on Windows). **Back** or <kbd>Esc</kbd> returns to where you were. Each Settings page is also in the command palette.
- **iPhone:** tap your initials at the top right of any tab, then **Settings**. Tap **Done** to close it.

## Job preferences

What you're looking for. Matching uses it to explain how well each job fits, and your first saved search starts from it.

- **Your name** (on Mac and Windows; on iPhone your name comes from your CV)
- **Roles**: the job titles you want.
- **Seniority**
- **Where**: the places you'd work in, and whether you want remote, hybrid or on-site work.
- **Salary floor**: the lowest pay you'd accept, with its currency and whether it's per year, month, day or hour.
- **Work authorization**: the countries (or the EU) where you may work, and on what basis.
- **Languages**, each with your level, from A1 to native.

None of it is required. Matches use your changes from the next search on.

## Mail accounts

Your connected inboxes, with their status, the folders checked and the job emails stored. Add an account, **Check now**, **Reconnect**, **Scan again** or **Disconnect…**. See [Mail](/guide/mail/).

## Notifications

Choose what to be told about: **Interview invites**, **Offers**, **Assessments**, **Decisions** (a short, quiet note when a company decides not to move forward; you can turn it off), **Automatic updates**, and **New matches** from your saved searches.

- **Quiet hours** pause notifications at night, from and to the times you choose. Everything still shows up in the app.
- On Mac and Windows, **Show notifications on this computer** shows them while BruteForceHR is open. If your system has notifications turned off for BruteForceHR, allow them there first.
- On iPhone, if notifications are off in iOS, **Open iOS Settings** takes you there.

## Job sources

Every job site that Jobs searches, whether it's working, when it last found jobs and how many postings it returned. Some sources need an access key that the app's owner sets up; until then they're marked **Not in use**. See [Jobs](/guide/jobs/#where-jobs-come-from).

## Companies you follow

Follow a company to get its new openings in Jobs without searching. Choose **Follow a company**, enter its name and the address of its careers page (for example `https://jobs.lever.co/acme`), and choose **Follow**. Each company shows how many open jobs it has and when it was last checked. **Stop following** can be undone.

## AI

- **Use AI features**: reading imported CVs, suggesting CV wording and explaining job matches. When it's off, you do these yourself.
- **Allow sending email text to AI**: lets Claude sort your job emails, so fewer of them ask for your review. When it's off, emails are sorted with rules only. It needs **Use AI features** on.

AI runs on BruteForceHR's server with Anthropic's Claude, and only for the features you turn on.

## Language and appearance

- **Language**: English, Turkish or German, or the same as your device. On Mac and Windows it's under **Settings › General**; on iPhone, **Language** opens the app's page in iOS Settings. The language also applies to notifications on your phone.
- **Appearance**: **System**, **Light** or **Dark**, per device.

## Updates

- **Mac and Windows:** **Settings › General** shows your version and **Check for updates**. The app checks every few hours by itself. Under **Update channel**, **Stable** gets tested versions; **Beta** gets new versions first, which may have rough edges.
- **iPhone:** updates arrive through TestFlight. **Settings › About** shows your version.

## Data & privacy

Export everything or delete your account. See [Data & privacy](/guide/data-and-privacy/).

## Sign out

- **Mac:** **BruteForceHR › Sign Out** in the menu bar, or search "Sign out" in the command palette.
- **Windows:** **File › Sign Out**, or the command palette.
- **iPhone:** tap your initials, then **Sign out**.

Signing out doesn't delete anything. Sign in again with the same email address to pick up where you left off.
