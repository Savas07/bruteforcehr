# Design status

Tracks the design protocol (BRIEF §5.5) per screen and platform. Audited against `docs/design/critiques/**` on 2026-10-24: in every row the round number links the critique folder and the A11y cell links the accessibility review. No UI change merges unless its row is approved (BRIEF §5.6).

- **References:** research doc for the area (`docs/design/research/<area>.md`).
- **Critique rounds:** the latest saved round in `docs/design/critiques/<platform>/<screen>/r<N>.md`; macOS and Windows share the `desktop/` folder. `—` means the row has no folder of its own (covered elsewhere, or N/A).
- **A11y:** `—` not run · `pass` · `fail`. "In rN" means the review is the Accessibility section of that round rather than a separate `accessibility.md` / `a11y.md`.
- **Status:** `not started` · `researching` (research doc complete; design not started) · `exploration` · `refinement` · `final` (latest round has zero Critical and zero unresolved Moderate findings and the accessibility review passes; owner sign-off pending) · `approved` (the owner approved it: the [M0 checkpoint](design/checkpoint-m0.md) and the legal pages) · `N/A` (a recorded decision removes the screen; the decision id is linked).

Synthesis (M0): [IA](design/IA.md) · [flows](design/flows.md) · [decisions](design/decisions.md) · [components](design/components.md) · [design system](design/system.md) · tokens in `packages/ui-tokens`.
