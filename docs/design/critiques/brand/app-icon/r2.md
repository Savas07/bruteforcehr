## Design Critique: BruteForceHR app icon (B monogram)

### Overall Impression

Calm, legible, clearly the same object from the 1024 App Store image down to a 16 px browser tab. The pixel-grid masters fixed the grey half-tone strokes of r1; the Liquid Glass renditions look native next to system icons. One issue showed up only in the Icon Composer renders (translucent glyph at 29 pt) and was fixed in this round.

### Usability (recognition at real sizes)

| Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Severity    | Recommendation                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Icon Composer's default group translucency (0.5) lets the teal background through the white B; at 29 pt (Settings, notifications) the glyph turned grey-teal and lost about a third of its contrast.                                              | 🟡 Moderate | Lower translucency to 0.2: the B stays white, the specular edge and shadow keep the Liquid Glass depth. |
| 16 px (favicon, ICO, ICNS 16): 2 px stroke on whole pixels, both counters 2 px and open, no half-tone columns (8× zoom). 24 and 32 px likewise crisp.                                                                                             | —           | Pass.                                                                                                   |
| macOS 16 px body is 14 px (1 px inset) and drops the shadow; the B is 8 × 10 px. It reads as a B in Finder list view, matching how system apps simplify at 16 px.                                                                                 | 🟢 Minor    | Accept.                                                                                                 |
| `favicon.svg` uses the 16 px geometry at every size; at 32 px (Retina tabs) the stroke is 12.5 % of the plate instead of 11 %. Slightly bolder than the app icons, invisible at tab size.                                                         | 🟢 Minor    | Accept: one SVG stays crisp at 16 and at 2×.                                                            |
| Tinted Dark (system-derived): the B takes the user's tint on a near-black plate; with a dark tint (purple in the sheet) the glyph sits at roughly 2.5:1. Icon Composer can't override the tinted background, and every app shares this behaviour. | 🟢 Minor    | Log. The glyph is the brightest layer, which is what the system tints most strongly.                    |

### Visual Hierarchy

- **What draws the eye first**: the white B; nothing competes (no badge-like details, no second colour).
- **Reading flow**: single mark; the lower bowl is now visibly larger, so the letter sits on a stable base instead of looking top-heavy.
- **Emphasis**: the glyph occupies 62.5 % of the canvas height, inside Apple's safe area on iOS and inside the 824 body on macOS.

### Consistency

| Element  | Issue                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Recommendation |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Colour   | Light: gradient `#2B827B → #1B5954` (tokens `accent.solid` dark → `accent.hover` light). Dark: `#1E2229 → #0E1014` plate (`surface.elevated` / `surface.canvas` dark) with a `#43BEB4` B (`accent.text` dark). | —              |
| Shape    | iOS/macOS 26: system shape from `AppIcon.icon`. macOS 13–15: Apple's 824/185.4 body + shadow. Windows/Linux/web: 3/16 radius plate. The website's header mark and favicon use the same plate.                  | —              |
| Geometry | One construction (centre-line spine, two semicircular bowls, round outer corners) at 1024, 32, 24 and 16; the small masters keep the bowl ratio within a pixel.                                                | —              |

### Accessibility

- **Color contrast** (SC 1.4.11 as a guide; logos are exempt): white B on the light plate 4.58:1 (top) to 8.07:1 (bottom); dark appearance `#43BEB4` on `#1E2229` 7.04:1 and on `#0E1014` 8.40:1.
- **Icon edge on dark canvases** (carried from r1): plate bottom `#1B5954` vs `#0E1014` 2.36:1, top 4.15:1. The B and the top edge carry the shape; macOS 26 and iOS 26 draw their own glass rim around the icon. 🟢 Minor, logged.
- **Text readability**: n/a. The name always accompanies the icon (Home Screen label, Dock tooltip, taskbar tooltip, tab title).

### What Works Well

- One silhouette at every size, so recognition transfers from the website favicon to the Dock and the Home Screen.
- The dark appearance is a designed variant (teal B on a near-black plate), not the system's automatic darkening.
- Assets are generated from four SVGs by one script, so a future tweak changes every platform at once.

### Priority Recommendations

1. **Lower the glass translucency to 0.2** — keeps the B white at 29 pt.
2. Nothing else above Minor.
