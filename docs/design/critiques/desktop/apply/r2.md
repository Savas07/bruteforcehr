## Design Critique: Apply by email

### Overall Impression
The r1 gap is closed: at 1280 × 800 the footer now reads "Attaching: Lebenslauf — Deutsch" next to "Review email", so the user sees what goes out before reviewing. Offline keeps the composer fully usable (drafting and Review are local); only Send / "I sent it" wait for a connection, and the Review footer says so in one sentence ("You're offline. Your draft is saved.").

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first:** "Apply to Fernweh Reisen", then the prefilled address and its source line.
- **Reading flow:** unchanged; the footer status line now completes the summary (what's attached → Review).
- **Emphasis:** one primary per step ("Review email", then "Send to bewerbung@fernweh-reisen.de" / "I sent it").
