## Design Critique: Conflict

### Overall Impression
The banner is right: amber, not red; "Your edits here are safe"; three clear actions. Compare is where it went wrong: it showed the other device's text as deleted, and in the real app it compared the wrong versions.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| Real app (`macos/cv-studio/conflict-compare-light.png`): Compare diffs this device's text against an older revision ("2 lines added, nothing removed"), never showing the other device's "Remote change."; a moment later it claims "These two versions have the same text" because the screen fell back to this device's own text when the other revision hadn't arrived. The user is asked to choose between versions they can't see. This is what failed the E2E. | 🔴 Critical | Compare against the revision the CV now points at (the other device's), and while it hasn't synced show "Getting the other version…" instead of a false "same text". |
| The other device's lines are struck through on red with "−", this device's in green with "+". Neither is the old one; the styling pre-judges "theirs is wrong" and is alarming. | 🟡 Moderate | Conflict mode: tag each line "Other version" / "This version" (the banner's own words), neutral tints, no strike-through; summary "1 line only in the other version, 1 line only in this one". |
| The description "From the other device's version to yours." reads as a direction of change. | 🟡 Moderate | "The lines that differ, by section. Whichever version you keep, the other stays in Version history." |
| The diff list isn't keyboard-scrollable. | 🟡 Moderate | Shared fix with history. |
| The banner doesn't say which device or when (research: "{device}, {time}"). | 🟢 Minor | Revisions don't record a device yet; add when they do. |
| At 1280 the banner takes ~170 px of the editor header and "Compare" wraps to its own line. | 🟢 Minor | Acceptable for a rare, important state. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: the amber banner title "This CV changed on another device". Correct.
- **Emphasis**: "Keep this version" primary, "Use the other version" secondary, "Compare" plain; the dialog repeats the first two.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Wording | Line tags reuse the banner's "this version" / "the other version". | — |
| Tone | Banner `InlineAlert` warning (role=status). | — |

### Accessibility
- **Non-colour cues**: tags are visible text; sr-only colon after the tag.
- **Live regions**: banner is `role="status"`; the loading line is `role="status"`.

### What Works Well
- Neither choice loses anything: the losing side stays in Version history, and the copy says so.

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Compare the right two versions, never claim "same" before both are here.**
2. **Neutral, source-labelled lines in conflict mode.**

## Resolution

| # | Finding | Severity | Status |
|---|---------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Wrong / missing "other" side in Compare | Critical | Fixed in two parts. | 4 | Diff not keyboard-scrollable | Moderate | Fixed (shared with history). |
| 5 | Device/time in banner | Minor | Logged. |
| 6 | Banner height at 1280 | Minor | Logged. |
