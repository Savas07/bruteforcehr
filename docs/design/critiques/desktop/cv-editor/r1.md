Context: CVs screen (`/cvs`), user goal "get my CV ready for this application, quickly, without the AI putting words in my mouth". Persona: active job seeker, often stressed and short on time, switching between phone and desktop. Stage: refinement. Focus: the four-pane layout at 1280 × 800 (the most common laptop window) and the AI tracked-change flow.

## Design Critique: CV editor (list · editor · preview · inspector)

### Overall Impression
The structure reads at once: CVs on the left, the form in the middle, the real page on the right, and at 1920 the inspector with the suggestion queue. The preview is honest (real text, standard headings, true page breaks). The biggest opportunity: at 1280 × 800 the AI suggestions, the reason the user opened the screen today, are invisible above the fold and nothing says they exist.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| At 1280 the inspector is closed and the first tracked change sits ~900 px below the fold inside an expanded item. Nothing in the toolbar says "3 suggestions to review", so a stressed user may never see them. | 🔴 Critical | Add a toolbar button "3 suggestions" (accent-subtle, sparkle glyph, F8 hint) that opens the Suggestions tab; collapsed items keep their "1 suggestion" badge. |
| Every item with a suggestion plus the first role open expanded by default; the form starts as a wall of fields and the user scrolls past 30 inputs to find anything. | 🟡 Moderate | Start with items collapsed (card shows title, org · dates, first bullet, suggestion badge). Expansion follows the user (click, F8, Show in CV, preview click). |
| The "Stronger verb" diff interleaves single kept words between deletions and insertions ("~~Maintained~~ Raised the Lighthouse accessibility score of customer-facing booking forms ~~and raised their Lighthouse accessibility score~~ to 98."), which is hard to read. | 🟡 Moderate | Absorb kept runs of one or two words between two changes into the change, so the diff reads as phrase → phrase. |
| Suggestions tab card header wraps "Add a result" onto two lines and truncates the location ("Bull…"). | 🟡 Moderate | Mode label on the first line, location on its own line below. |
| Group heading in the Suggestions tab is "Experience · 3", which reads like the section's role count shown in the editor ("Experience · 3"). | 🟢 Minor | Group heading is the section name only; the count is already in "3 suggestions to review". |
| Section jump nav clips the last entries ("Talks", "Certificates") at 1280 with no affordance that it scrolls. | 🟢 Minor | Logged: the nav scrolls horizontally and every section is also reachable by Tab; revisit with a fade edge if users miss it. |
| Error state shows only the title and the pane error; the list pane chrome is gone. | 🟢 Minor | Logged: states.md asks for chrome to render, but with nothing cached there is no list to show; the title stays. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: the CV title "Base CV — English" and the selected list row (teal tint). Correct for orientation, but the pending suggestions should be the second thing, and today they aren't on screen at 1280.
- **Reading flow**: list → title + save status → jump nav → Summary → Experience. Good left-to-right; the preview on the right confirms the result.
- **Emphasis**: the inline tracked change (accent gutter, underlined insertions, struck deletions, primary "Use suggestion") is the strongest element when visible, which is right. The preview's page shadow on the sunken canvas reads as "output", as intended.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Item and section move buttons | Item rows and section headers use the same ↑ / ↓ icon buttons; consistent. | Keep. |
| Pills | Language pill (list) and "Latest" tag (history) use the same small radius and caption size. | Keep. |
| Disclaimer | "Suggested by Claude…" appears under every inline change and once in the tab: consistent with D13. | Keep. |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: token text on surfaces passes (tokens are AA-tested); insertions use underline + tint and deletions strike-through, so colour is never the only cue. The disabled "Use suggestion" explains why in visible text.
- **Touch targets**: 24 px icon buttons in dense rows (desktop minimum per tokens); 32 px for form controls.
- **Text readability**: form 15 px callout; the preview at 40 % is a page overview, not reading text (zoom controls and Mod+= are there).

### What Works Well
- The placeholder "[X%]" becomes an amber inline field and the button says exactly why it's disabled: "Fill in [X%] with your real numbers first." Calm, and it keeps the AI from inventing a number.
- The preview is the real document, light even in dark mode, with A4 page breaks, German month names on the German variant and standard headings.
- Offline: "Saved on this device · Updated 10:05" plus the shell strip; nothing blocks editing.
- Empty state: two equal choice cards with what each needs ("PDF, DOCX, JSON Resume or the LinkedIn ZIP", "about 10 min").

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Make pending suggestions discoverable at every width** — toolbar "3 suggestions" button opening the Suggestions tab, F8 jump.
2. **Start with items collapsed** — less scrolling, the card summaries do the job.
3. **Cleaner phrase-level diffs and a non-wrapping queue card header.**

## Resolution

| # | Finding | Severity | Status |
|---|---------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Suggestions not discoverable at 1280 | Critical | Fixed (commit below): toolbar "n suggestions" button opens the inspector on Suggestions. |
| 2 | Items expanded by default | Moderate | Fixed: items start collapsed; badges show pending suggestions. |
| 3 | Interleaved diff | Moderate | Fixed: `wordDiff` absorbs kept runs ≤ 2 words between changes (unit-tested). |
| 4 | Queue card header wrapping | Moderate | Fixed: location on its own line. |
| 5 | Group heading count | Minor | Fixed: section name only. |
| 6 | Jump nav clipping | Minor | Logged (scrolls; Tab reaches every section). |
| 7 | Error state without list chrome | Minor | Rejected: nothing cached to list; title stays per states.md. |
