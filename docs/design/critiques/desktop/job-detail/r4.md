## Design Critique: Jobs list rows

### Overall Impression
Before, the posting age ("yesterday") and saved mark took a ~60 px column down the whole row, so the salary line wrapped ("€65,000–€78,000 a year · / employer") on every row with a stated salary in macOS at 1280: rows were a line taller than needed, and fewer jobs fit on the first screen. After, that column sits beside the title and company only; the salary line and the pills use the full text width.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| Before: the salary + provenance line wraps to two lines at 1280 (macOS, all € / £ rows; r1 #12, r3 #4). | 🟢 Minor | **Fixed**: CSS grid row (monogram · text · age column spanning the title and company rows only); salary and pills span the text and age columns. |
| After: six-digit US$ ranges ("US$115,000–US$140,000 a year · employer") still break once on macOS at 1280, at the "·" before the provenance; one line on Windows. | 🟢 Minor | Accept: the break falls between amount and provenance, and the full wording stays in the tooltip and accessible text. |
| The 1920 × 1080 macOS captures render narrower than 1920 (runner display); same before and after. | — | Capture limitation, not a design finding. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first:** the title, then the band pill. Unchanged.
- **Reading flow:** title → company · place → salary → band / status / sources. The source count now shares the band pill's line ("Strong match · 2 sources") instead of taking its own line.
- **Emphasis:** age stays top-right next to the title, the saved mark under it; rows got shorter (Pixelpost row: 7 lines → 5 in macOS light 1280).

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Row anatomy | Same Glassdoor anatomy and order as job-feed-search.md "Decision — Desktop"; the hover / focus row actions still cover the top-right corner only. | — |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast:** unchanged tokens (`fg-secondary` / `fg-tertiary` on the row surfaces).
- **Touch targets:** unchanged (row actions 24 px).
- **Text readability:** unchanged sizes; the accessible name is still title + company, the salary's screen-reader text is still the full "· from employer" form. DOM order unchanged (grid placement only).

### What Works Well
- The fix is layout only: no copy or data change, so TR / DE keep their translations.
- More rows per screen at 1280 (macOS light: 5 full rows instead of 4).

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Ship it in v0.1.1.**
2. **Revisit currency display** (e.g. "$" for the user's own market) if US$ rows bother the user.
