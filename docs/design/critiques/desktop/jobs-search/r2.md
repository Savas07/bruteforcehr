## Design Critique: Jobs search and filters — after r1

### Overall Impression
The capture now shows what its URL says: "react" in the field, 34 jobs, the Work mode popover open with "Show 34 jobs". The live search reads as one action — field and button side by side — and its banner says "No new jobs for “react”" with "Checked 90 postings, 30 already in your list. Skipped: Adzuna, Jooble, USAJOBS and Reed (no access key yet)."

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| 1. Running and done banners are both two lines (title + one sentence), so the list doesn't jump when the search finishes. | — | Pass. |
| 2. DE at 1280: the popover ("Arbeitsmodell", "Zurücksetzen", "34 Stellen anzeigen") fits its 288 px width; the bar is one line with "Datum" (job-detail r6 #2). | — | Pass. |
| 3. Offline: the popover still opens and filters (local data); Search job sites is disabled with "Needs a connection"; the offline bar sits above the header. | — | Pass. |
| 4. The running banner keeps the info glyph while the button shows the spinner; the banner itself doesn't animate. | 🟢 Minor | Accept: one spinner per action; the banner text says it is running ("Searching…"). |
| 5. While a popover is open, axe can't decide contrast for the list text it covers and lists the open pill's `aria-controls` as "needs review". | — | Not a finding: overlap and a portalled Radix popover; 0 violations. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first:** the open popover and its primary "Show 34 jobs"; with the banner, its bold info title.
- **Reading flow:** field → Search job sites → banner → list.
- **Emphasis:** "No new jobs" leads; the numbers are body text.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Banner | Same `InlineAlert` (info / warning, Dismiss) as other in-screen results. | — |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast:** unchanged tested pairings; axe 0 violations in all `jobs-search-*` reports (see `accessibility.md`).
- **Touch targets / readability:** unchanged from r1.

### What Works Well
- One polite live region, mounted before the search starts, receives "Searching job sites for “react”… New jobs show up in the list as they're found." and then the result (DOM checked in the harness; not yet heard with VoiceOver / NVDA).
- Closing the popover (Esc, outside click, Show n jobs) clears `?filters`, so reload and Back don't reopen it.
