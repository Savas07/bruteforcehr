## Design Critique: Connect your email

### Overall Impression
The steps now have one heading each and open where the user types, and the waiting step has a single way back. One small inconsistency remained in the scan result: two filled buttons.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| Scan result with emails to review: "Review in Mail" and the footer's "Done" are both filled, so the step has two primaries. | 🟢 Minor | "Review in Mail" stays filled; "Done" becomes secondary while it's offered. |
| Waiting: the inline "Cancel" (back to the intro) and the footer "Cancel" (close the dialog) did different things. | 🟢 Minor | Fixed during r1: the inline one is gone; Back returns to the picker, Cancel closes. Verified. |
| Other (IMAP): the IMAP server field starts empty with the port prefilled; the SMTP hint says empty means reading only. | — | Works as designed (connect-email.md "Other"). |
| Outlook not available yet: explains why an app password won't work (Microsoft turned them off for Outlook.com) and offers "Choose another account". | — | Calm and accurate; no change. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: the step title, then the focused field (password on the forms, the selected tile on the picker).
- **Reading flow**: top-down in the main column; the side column is reference (promises or provider steps).
- **Emphasis**: one filled button per step after the fix.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Step titles | Match the Settings pane names and the provider names in Settings › Mail accounts ("iCloud Mail", "GMX"). | — |
| Not-configured state | Neutral clock icon and text, like Flodesk/Ditto; never the error tone. | — |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: axe (WCAG 2.1 A/AA) clean in every state, both engines, both themes (accessibility.md).
- **Focus**: initial focus on the first empty field; after a failed test, focus moves to the first field to fix and the message is tied to it with `aria-describedby`.
- **Text readability**: German and Turkish captures wrap without clipping (accessibility.md, length check).

### What Works Well
- The scan result says what was found and where to go next in one line ("5 emails are waiting for your review." + Review in Mail).
- Reconnect opens straight on the form with the saved servers and asks only for a new app password.

### Priority Recommendations
1. **One primary in the scan result** — Done as secondary while Review in Mail is offered.

## Resolution

- Scan result: fixed (`MailConnectDialog.tsx`, the scanning step's footer).
- Open findings: none Critical, none Moderate.
