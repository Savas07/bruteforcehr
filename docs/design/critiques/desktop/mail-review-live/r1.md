## Design Critique: Mail review (live)

### Overall Impression
The live screen keeps the M0 layout and adds the pieces live data needs without new chrome: a "Suggested interview" card under the review card, "Open in Gmail/GMX…" only where we can open the inbox, and the application's emails beside its timeline. The correction popover is honest now: it says what the status will become instead of offering moves the server won't make.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| The invite shows its time twice: "Interview · Tomorrow, 17:00" in the review card's dates and "Tomorrow, 17:00 · 30 min" in the Suggested interview card right below. | 🟢 Minor | Log: the card's dates come from the classifier (all dates in the email), the suggestion is what Confirm will add (with length and place). Both are needed when they differ. |
| Correction popover: the Status row is now read-only ("Moves to Screening" / "No status change") with "For another status, move the application in the Tracker." | — | Matches the server (`mailMessageReviewed` applies forward moves and decisions only; clients write only the review fields). |
| Undo after the 10-second window: the toast's Undo (also ⌘Z) reverts the status move the server made; if the decision changed nothing, a calm info toast says so. | — | Works as designed (decisions.md D11). |
| "Open in your mail app" for IMAP servers we can't open was a dead end in M0. | — | Fixed: the button shows only for providers we can open (Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo, Yandex, GMX, WEB.DE). |
| Today: the running scan sits under the date as a compact checklist line with a bar; it disappears when the scan is done (the results are in To review). | — | Clear and quiet. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: the review card's headline and Confirm, as in M0; the Suggested interview card is secondary (sunken surface, no filled button while pending).
- **Reading flow**: proposal → reason → evidence → fields → actions → suggested interview → email text.
- **Emphasis**: "Add interview" is the only button in the interview card and only after an automatic change.
