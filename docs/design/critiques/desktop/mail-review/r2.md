## Design Critique: Mail review

### Overall Impression
The card and the details now agree ("Application: Sail Analytics · Product Engineer, Growth"), the correction popover opens beside the card so the proposal, reason and quote stay readable while correcting, and hovering a row no longer mangles its time. What remains is polish: the popover also covers part of the sidebar at 1280 px, and a few small repetitions.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| At 1280×800 the correction popover (400 px) extends past the list pane over the sidebar's Mail folders. Nothing there is needed while correcting, and Esc / Cancel return focus to the card. | 🟢 Minor | Log. A narrower popover would truncate application names again (r1 #11). |
| With no application chosen, the Status field used to list twelve greyed statuses without saying why (found while testing the Application → "No application" path). | 🟢 Minor | Show only "No status change" plus "Choose an application first". |
| Error state: the reading-pane line sat at the top-left corner, reading like a stray label. | 🟢 Minor | Center it in the pane. |
| Updates reading pane: the company · role line appears in the automatic-change card and again as the email header's sender line. | 🟢 Minor | Log: the change card is the shared `AutoChangeRow` (Today uses the same); the header is the email's own identity. Both carry meaning. |
| The re-auth callout stays four to five lines tall (five in Turkish) at the top of every folder. | 🟢 Minor | Log: states.md requires it to stay until the inbox is reconnected; the copy is already title + one sentence. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: the selected row and the card headline, then the amber callout. Correct for a review queue.
- **Reading flow**: unchanged from r1 and still top-down; the popover now reads left-to-right next to the card it edits (fields summary → search → options → Save correction).
- **Emphasis**: one filled button per surface (Confirm on the card, Save correction in the popover). Pending rows use a semibold sender; reviewed rows show a quiet "Confirmed" / "Corrected" chip.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Turkish section name | The shell sidebar says "E-posta" while the Mail screen title (and IA.md's destination table) say "Posta". | Log for Main: one of the two shell / screen strings should change; the screen follows IA.md. |
| Keyboard hint bar | Now "C Confirm · M Change · N Not job mail · H Review later" — matches the buttons and the shortcuts sheet. | — |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: no new pairs this round; dark-mode selected row (teal) keeps sender, classification and subject legible. Full audit in accessibility.md.
- **Touch targets**: unchanged (rows ≥ 64 px, buttons 24–32 px, keyboard equivalents for the 24 px hover icons).
- **Text readability**: TR strings wrap without clipping in rows, the callout, the card and the hint bar at 1280×800.

### What Works Well
- The card and the rail now tell one story; "This email doesn't change a status." is honest when there's nothing to move.
- Correcting is fully keyboard-driven (M, type, Enter, Tab, ⌘↩) and the email stays visible.
- After every decision focus moves to the next email and the toast says what happened in the past tense with Undo; clearing the queue lands on a calm "Nothing to review" with "See recent updates".
- Turkish fits: "Yeniden bağlanın", "Sonra inceleyin", "İş e-postası değil" sit on one line each.

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Align the Turkish section name** across shell and screen (owner: Main / shell).
2. **Keep an eye on the popover width** when real application names get long (it already wraps).
3. **Revisit the Updates header duplication** once Today and Mail share real data.
