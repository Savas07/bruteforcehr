# Settings (desktop) — critique round 1

Skill: `design-critique`. Context: the new desktop Settings (`/settings/$pane`), opened from the sidebar row, ⌘, / Ctrl+, the app menu and the palette. Panes: Mail accounts, Notifications, Job sources, Companies you follow, AI, General (Data & privacy joins from AccountData). Who: the same job seeker, setting things once and checking health now and then. Stage: refinement (first build). Research: settings-account.md "Decision — Desktop" (Teams/Google Drive/Buffer shell, Notion Mail/Salesforce connections, Slack/Vanta/Asana notifications, ElevenLabs/incident.io sources, Twenty/Clerk/Buffer updates).

Captures judged (browser harness, design build, WebKit, 1280×800, light and dark): every pane populated; Mail accounts with `?scan=failed`; Companies.

## Design Critique: Settings

### Overall Impression
A quiet, familiar settings shell: nav on the left, one column of rows with On/Off text next to each switch, autosave. Two things cost clarity: the Companies list has loose, uneven spacing, and Mail accounts repeats the same Spam/Junk line in every card.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| Companies you follow: each row ends with an empty, reserved line for the "Saved" status, so rows are ~30 px taller than their content and the list looks unfinished. | 🟡 Moderate | The status region stays mounted for announcements but takes no height while empty. |
| Mail accounts: "We also check Spam/Junk…" is repeated in every account card (three times with the scanning inbox). | 🟡 Moderate | Say it once, in the section's description. |
| Failed scan in a card: "We couldn't finish scanning…" + "Try the scan again" sits between the status and the footer of the card; readable, never red. | — | Works as designed (connect-email.md failure copy). |
| Job sources are read-only and say who manages the keys. No dead "Add key" buttons. | — | Correct for project-level secrets. |
| Notifications: "New matches" helper says "on your phone" while the section says phone + this computer; this computer notifies only for job emails. | 🟢 Minor | Log: accurate as written; the device switch's helper says "For new job emails". |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: the pane title, then the first section heading and its first control. Correct.
- **Reading flow**: title → helper → control per row; switches carry their state as text (Teams).
- **Emphasis**: the only filled buttons are actions that need doing (Reconnect on a paused inbox); "Add email account" and "Follow a company" are secondary at the section's end.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Health text | Mail accounts, job sources and companies all use icon + text (check, amber alert, spinner), never color alone, never red. | — |
| Section headings | Pane title (h1) and section titles (h2) never repeat each other (Companies' section is "Careers pages"). | — |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: axe clean on every pane (accessibility.md).
- **Touch targets**: switches 36×20 inside a larger button with the On/Off label; menus 32 px.
- **Text readability**: helpers wrap to two lines at 1280 px.

### What Works Well
- Quiet hours as one sentence ("From 22:30 to 8:00") with the zone named.
- Companies show what we found (open jobs, checked when) or why not, in plain words.
- General keeps channel caveats from the research ("They might have rough edges…").

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Tighten the Companies rows** — no reserved height for the save status.
2. **Say Spam/Junk once** — move it to the section description.

## Resolution

- Companies spacing: fixed (`save.tsx`: the live region has no minimum height).
- Spam/Junk: fixed (`MailAccountsPane.tsx`, section description).
- "New matches" helper: logged, unchanged.
