## Design Critique: Sidebar navigation (desktop) — round 2 (real apps)

Context: the sidebar in the real Tauri shells. Captures judged: every destination capture, e.g. `today-{light,dark}-{1280x800,1920x1080}.png`, per OS. CI run [40118277736](https://github.com/Savas07/bruteforcehr/actions/runs/40118277736) (commit `c4cc52a`), artifacts `desktop-screens-{macos,windows}-latest` from `pnpm capture` (e2e/capture/capture.e2e.ts): the real apps (WKWebView on macOS, WebView2 on Windows), light + dark, 1280 × 800 and 1920 × 1080. Windows' hosted display is 1024 × 768, so its captures are zoomed out (e2e/capture/helpers.ts). The same run's E2E suite (`e2e/specs/app.e2e.ts`) passed on both OSes.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
