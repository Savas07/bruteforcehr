## Design Critique: Sign-in with an email link (desktop) — round 2

Context: as in [r1](r1.md), after its fixes. Focus: Turkish (siz) and German (du), keyboard flow and the screen-reader view.

Captures judged:

- `capture:design sign-in --axe`: 18 states × WebKit + Chromium × light + dark (EN, 72 PNGs) after the r1 fixes.
- `capture:design sign-in --axe --locale tr|de --themes light`: 18 states × both engines per language (72 PNGs, `artifacts/screens/desktop/harness/{webkit,chromium}/{tr,de}/sign-in-*.png`).
- A Playwright keyboard probe in both engines (no pointer): Tab order per state, submit with Enter, an invalid address, send → waiting, Esc, the accessibility tree (`ariaSnapshot`) of the waiting and expired states, and a 640×400 viewport (≈ 1280×800 at 200 % zoom).

### Overall Impression

The r1 fixes hold. Turkish and German fit the 400 px column without truncation, and the keyboard path from address to "Check your email" and back needs no pointer.

### Visual Hierarchy

- DE: "Schau in dein Postfach" + three body lines, and "Anmeldelink senden" fits on one line. The legal line wraps to two lines and stays secondary.
- TR: "E-postanızı kontrol edin", with the address first ("… adresine bir giriş bağlantısı gönderdik"). Bold still lands on the address.

### Consistency

| Element   | Issue                                                                                                                                    | Recommendation |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Formality | DE uses du everywhere ("Gib deine E-Mail-Adresse ein", "Schick dir einen neuen"). TR uses siz in sentences and in buttons after the fix. | —              |
| Terms     | "sign-in link" / "giriş bağlantısı" / "Anmeldelink" are used the same way in every state.                                                | —              |

### Accessibility

- axe WCAG 2.1 A/AA: 0 violations in all EN, TR and DE states, in both engines and themes.
- Keyboard (both engines): the email step opens with focus in the field. Tab goes field → Send sign-in link → Terms → Privacy Policy. An invalid address keeps focus in the field with `aria-invalid="true"` and the message as its description. After sending, focus is on the "Check your email" heading. Error states focus their heading. The confirm step focuses its field.
- 640×400: no horizontal overflow, and the column scrolls vertically.
