### Overall Impression
Round-1 fixes hold: To review now reads as "3 decisions, then 3 things we did for you", with no proposed status shown as fact. Remaining issues are about the edges: what sits under the Undo toast and how sparse two detail panes feel.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| The Undo toast (bottom-left of the content area) covers the last rows of the list; with "Done today" expanded its first entry sits under the toast and can't be scrolled clear. | 🟡 Moderate | Give the list enough bottom padding to scroll everything above the toast. |
| In the detail pane, automatic changes and follow-ups start straight with the shared card's h3 (no h2), and the card floats without context ("what am I looking at?"). | 🟡 Moderate | A quiet h2 kicker above the card: "Updated automatically" / "Suggested follow-up". |
| Right-clicking a row selects the word under the pointer before the menu opens. | 🟢 Minor | `select-none` on rows. |
| TR/DE: Today strings were English fallbacks in the detail pane, menus and toasts. | 🟡 Moderate | Translate all `today.*` ids (TR siz, DE du); no placeholder suffixes in TR. |
| First run: the agenda rail at ≥ 1440 px shows only "No interviews this week" next to the connect-email card. | 🟢 Minor | states.md asks the rail to keep its own line; keep. |
| The auto-change detail is short (one card), leaving the pane mostly empty. | 🟢 Minor | Acceptable: everything needed (source, reason, Undo, View source, Mark as seen) is there; a timeline excerpt can come with Tracker data in M2. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: Next up card, then the To review heading with its plain "· 3".
- **Reading flow**: top-down through fixed sections; the sub-heading "Updated automatically · you can undo these" correctly lowers the weight of FYIs.
- **Emphasis**: filled teal only on the primary action of the pane (Confirm / Mark as done / Open in Jobs); pills only for applied statuses and match bands.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Date formats | WebKit (en-US browser locale) shows "Today, 03:00 PM"; Chromium "15:00" | Data, not design: formatting follows the locale (`format.ts`); TR/DE captures show 24 h |
| Section titles vs pane headings | Consistent (callout semibold / title3) | — |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: no new colors; tokens only. Axe run in the accessibility review.
- **Touch targets**: unchanged (rows ≥ 56 px; buttons 32 px).
- **Text readability**: DE titles wrap to two lines ("Sieht so aus, als bräuchte Tannenbaum Software weitere Informationen") without truncation; TR first-run buttons stack (EmptyState rule) and stay readable.

### What Works Well
- TR and DE layouts hold at 1280×800: no clipped pills, the Open/Later control fits ("Açık · Sonra", "Offen · Später").
- The Later view explains itself ("Snoozed until tomorrow · It comes back to Today then") with a one-click way back.
- Context menus mirror the keys (E, O, C, M, N, H, D, S) with keycaps.

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Clear the toast** — nothing actionable may sit under it.
2. **Name the shared cards in the pane** — structure for screen readers, context for everyone.
3. **Ship TR/DE** — the user switches locales; fallbacks look broken.
