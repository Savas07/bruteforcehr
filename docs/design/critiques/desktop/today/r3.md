### Overall Impression
The screen is settled: calm, ordered, honest about what happened automatically. The one remaining moderate issue was a layout jump from loading to ready at ≥ 1200 px.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| At ≥ 1200 px the loading state renders a centered single column, then the ready state snaps into list + detail panes: the skeleton isn't shape-matched to what follows (states.md), and the title moves. | 🟡 Moderate | Render the split layout while loading, with row skeletons in the list and a gated text skeleton in the detail pane. |
| Inline expansion (< 1200 px) of a follow-up repeats company, role and message already shown in its row (the shared `NudgeCard` carries its own header). | 🟢 Minor | Acceptable at narrow widths: the card is the same component the Tracker uses; a `compact` variant without header would be a shared-component change. Logged. |
| Offline: pending rows show the sync glyph next to their time; the header shows "Updated 10:05"; actions stay enabled (queued). Join is disabled with "Needs a connection" when it applies. | — | Works as states.md describes. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: the title, then Next up. In dark, the selected row's teal tint is the strongest surface, which matches where the keyboard is.
- **Reading flow**: unchanged and correct.
- **Emphasis**: offline strip is neutral, not red; good.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Skeletons | Rail and list skeletons follow the real shapes; detail now too | — |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: tokens only; checked by axe in the accessibility review.
- **Touch targets**: unchanged.
- **Text readability**: unchanged.

### What Works Well
- Offline is quiet and specific (strip, sidebar sync line, per-row glyph, "Updated 10:05").
- Inline mode keeps the keyboard map: Enter expands, Esc collapses back to the row, H opens the snooze menu on the expanded card.

### Priority Recommendations
