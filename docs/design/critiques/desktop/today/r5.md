## Design Critique: Today (desktop) — round 5 (Today-live)

Context: M3 moves Today onto the user's stored tracker data (`buildTodayModel(ScreenData)` through `useTrackerModel`; actions through `useTrackerActions`). Scope: the real-data states only — follow-ups answered and snoozed, the Later view, reminder deadlines (`collectDeadlines` kind `reminder`), Done today built from the data, and the empty Today a production M3 user sees (no mail account, no matches, no job feed until M4/M5). Persona and stage as rounds 1–4; stage: refinement before release.

### Research (Mobbin, web)
Queries: "snoozed items list showing when each item comes back" (8 candidates), "empty state explaining a feature is coming soon without a button" (6 candidates). Kept:
- [ClickUp — Inbox › Later](https://mobbin.com/screens/bc1ba74d-c344-40c0-b43b-c2815a956764): snoozed items in their own tab, each row ends with "Unsnoozed on Oct 24". → our Later rows end with "Back on Fri 23 Oct" in the meta slot.
- [Amie — Work list "Later" group](https://mobbin.com/screens/05c4146b-77d3-486d-809d-d8173d8aa4d6): deferred items grouped under one heading, a small date chip per row, no extra chrome. → one "Snoozed" section, date in the row meta, no badges.
- [Dialpad — Scheduled](https://mobbin.com/screens/8f4a5d28-c37d-4f61-8e18-56e81f265316): "Send Tomorrow at 12:00 pm" under the title. → relative day + clock for the detail notice and the agenda.
- [Eventbrite — delivery report empty](https://mobbin.com/screens/3ef69862-4fa7-440c-8cbe-2503ea5907f8): "No delivery information yet … Check back later for updates." — explains why it's empty and when that changes, no dead-end button.
- [Google Analytics — data collection pending](https://mobbin.com/screens/12d45d8a-28f9-423a-bd90-28fee871b737): says what's pending and offers the one action that works now ("Continue to Home").
- [PlanetScale — Beta features empty](https://mobbin.com/screens/9a716b9b-b6cc-4058-ba17-fafbd4d2e907): calm one-line card, no action when nothing can be done.
Not kept: Hex scheduled runs, HubSpot scheduled email, Asana My tasks, Fresha appointments, Circle posts (tables of scheduled items, not deferral); Deputy, Curater, Partiful (marketing tone or illustration-led).

### Overall Impression
The real-data Today reads like the M0 design, which is the point: answered suggestions leave, come back with Undo and show up in Done today; the Later view is a short, calm list. The first pass had two trust problems (a snooze that said "tomorrow" and showed another day, and reminders repeating the interview right above them) and an empty state whose button led nowhere in this version.

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: Next up hero card (populated); the briefcase glyph and title (empty). Correct.
- **Reading flow**: Later view is one heading, one row per suggestion with "Back on …" right-aligned where other rows show their time; the detail opens with the info notice and "Move back now", then the suggestion card. Same slots as the open list, so nothing new to learn.
- **Emphasis**: Done today stays collapsed and grey; "Follow-up not needed · now" in footnote tertiary. Reminders use the same deadline row and chip as the others; only the detail label differs.

### UX Copy: first-use card (production M3)
**Recommended**: title "Add the jobs you've applied to" · body "Today then shows your interviews, follow-ups and reminders. Email invites and replies come in a later update." · button "Add application ⌘N".

| Option | Copy | Tone | Best For |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| A (chosen) | "Add the jobs you've applied to" + "…Email invites and replies come in a later update." | Calm, task-first | M3: one working action, honest about email |
| B | "Connect your email to see interview invites and replies here." + "Email connection arrives in a later update." (no button) | Explains, no action | When adding applications isn't possible either |
| C | "Nothing here yet" + "Add an application to get started." | Generic | — (doesn't say what Today is for) |

Rationale: what this is + why it's empty + how to start (ux-copy "Empty states"); no date promised for email; no guilt. Localization: TR siz ("Başvurduğunuz işleri ekleyin", "Başvuru ekleyin"), DE du ("Füge die Jobs hinzu, auf die du dich beworben hast", "Bewerbung hinzufügen"); DE title runs ~45 % longer and wraps on two lines at 1280 px, fine.

### Accessibility
axe (WCAG 2.1 A/AA) on the 12 probe states: 0 critical · 0 serious · 0 moderate · 0 minor. After "Not needed" the selection moves to the next row (Quill Docs → Northstar Maps in the capture), as in round 1's focus fix; the Later detail notice is an `InlineAlert` (`role="status"`, not alert) with "Move back now" after its title.

### What Works Well
- Undo restores exactly: Not needed and Keep waiting clear their date, the No response move is reverted with an event, Mark as done puts the reminder text and date back, Move back now restores the previous snooze.
- Done today now reflects the whole day on every device (notes, follow-ups logged, answers, undone moves), not one session.
- To review and New matches simply aren't there without mail and matches: no zero counts, no "coming soon" sections.

### Priority Recommendations
1. **One clock for snoozes** — the toast, the row and the notice must agree.
2. **No duplicate reminders** — one date, one row.
3. **No dead-end first-use action** — offer the action that works in this version.
