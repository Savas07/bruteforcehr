## Design Critique: Tracker board (desktop)

### Overall Impression
Calm and legible: grey wells, white cards and one teal accent make the pipeline readable at a glance, and the peek of the fourth column signals horizontal scroll without a scrollbar. The biggest opportunity is in the drag moment: the page itself slides sideways while dragging, and the Closed drop zones push the board around under the pointer.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| While dragging a card towards the right edge, the whole window content scrolls horizontally (the sidebar slides out of view and the board shifts left). dnd-kit auto-scrolls every scrollable ancestor, including the shell. | 🔴 Critical | Limit auto-scroll to the board's own scroller (`autoScroll.canScroll`). |
| When a drag starts, the 40 px Closed rail grows into a 272 px panel inside the flex row, so every column shrinks and the drop targets move under the pointer mid-gesture. | 🟡 Moderate | Overlay the five drop zones on the board's right edge (absolute, elevated) so nothing reflows. |
| Keyboard step (⇧⌘→) does nothing after closing the `M` menu with Esc: focus returns to the card's "Move to…" button, which isn't treated as "on a card". | 🟡 Moderate | Mark the card's list item (both buttons) as the card for the key bindings and track focus on it. |
| The envelope badge for automatic moves sits on the monogram's corner and covers the second initial ("N✉"), so neither reads well. | 🟡 Moderate | Show the envelope as an inline 14 px glyph after the company name, with the same spoken label. |
| Invalid drop zones (Accepted, Declined before an offer) both print "Accepted and Declined come after an offer." — the same sentence twice, stacked, while the user is mid-drag. | 🟡 Moderate | Dim both zones; show the reason only on the zone under the pointer. |
| Moving a card to Applied leaves "Applied: Not yet" in the detail summary. | 🟡 Moderate | When a move reaches Applied (or beyond) and no date is set, show the move's time, as `@bfhr/core` `transition` records it. |
| Turkish capture: the summary line under the title and all new board strings fall back to English ("18 active applications · 7 closed"). | 🟡 Moderate | Translate every `tracker-board.*` id in TR (siz) and DE (du). |
| At 1280 px with the detail panel open, the board keeps ~1.7 columns; the panel opens at its 480 px default. | 🟢 Minor | Acceptable for a 1280 window (panel is resizable to 400 px). Log; request a narrower default below 1440 from the shared `DetailSidePanel`. |
| Every expanded column shows a collapse « button; seven of them in a row add visual noise. | 🟢 Minor | Log. Keep (HubSpot pattern; discoverable collapse). Revisit if the user finds it noisy. |
| First use: seven empty wells each end with a "+ Add" ghost row. Heavy, but it is what states.md specifies and it teaches that every stage accepts adds. | 🟢 Minor | Log. Keep per states.md. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first:** the "Tracker" title, then the column headers with dots and counts. Correct: the user orients by stage first.
- **Reading flow:** left to right across stages, top to bottom inside a column (next-action order). The peek column and the Closed rail close the row on the right.
- **Emphasis:** the role line is the strongest text on a card, company secondary, meta and staleness tertiary. Right for scanning. Staleness ("No reply in 9 days") is calm grey, not a warning colour: good for the tone.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Status tones | Column dots use the phase tones; closed outcomes are neutral grey, Accepted gentle green, "Not selected" never red. Consistent with system.md. | — |
| Toast | Uses the shared toast with ⌘Z / Ctrl+Z keycaps and "Restored". Consistent. | — |
| Timeline | Shared `TimelineRow`: automatic changes show "Automatic · Mail", the email reason, Undo and View source; undone rows get the "Undone" tag. | — |
| Card corner button | Appears on hover / focus only; matches the IconButton style elsewhere. | — |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast:** card text on `surface.base` and secondary text on `surface.sunken` read clearly in light and dark; verify with axe in the a11y pass.
- **Touch targets:** card buttons are full-card; the corner "Move to…" button and collapse buttons are 24 px (desktop minimum).
- **Text readability:** callout/footnote sizes, two-line role clamp, no truncation of status labels. DE/TR headers fit in 272 px.

### What Works Well
- The Closed rail keeps five outcomes one drop away without five extra columns; rejections read "Not selected" in grey.
- The detail panel puts the automatic Offer move first with its source and a one-click Undo, and the Undo writes a visible "Undid a move · back to Interviewing" row.
- The drag header states the exact transition ("Applied → Screening").

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Stop the window from scrolling during drag** — it breaks orientation at the moment the user needs it most.
2. **Overlay the Closed drop zones** — targets must stay still under the pointer.
3. **Make keyboard steps work from either card button** — keyboard parity is a promise of the desktop app.

## Resolution

| # | Finding | Severity | Resolution |
|---|---------|----------|------------|
| 1 | Window scrolls during drag | Critical | **Fixed** in `93fe889`: `autoScroll={{ canScroll: (element) => element === boardRef.current }}`. |
| 2 | Closed rail reflows the board when a drag starts | Moderate | **Fixed** in `93fe889`: drop zones are an absolutely positioned, elevated overlay on the board's right edge. |
| 3 | ⇧⌘→ ignored after Esc from the `M` menu | Moderate | **Fixed** in `93fe889`: `data-card-id` and focus tracking moved to the card's list item. |
| 4 | Envelope badge covers monogram initials | Moderate | **Fixed** in `93fe889`: inline 14 px envelope after the company name, spoken label kept. |
| 5 | Duplicate reason on invalid drop zones | Moderate | **Fixed** in `93fe889`: reason only on the hovered zone; both stay dimmed. |
| 6 | "Applied: Not yet" after moving to Applied | Moderate | **Fixed** in `93fe889`: `applyBoardMoves` sets `summary.appliedAt` when a move reaches Applied or beyond without a date. |
| 7 | TR/DE strings missing | Moderate | **Fixed** in `93fe889`: 42 ids translated (TR siz, DE du); `i18n:check` reports none untranslated for `tracker-board.*`. |
| 8 | Detail panel squeezes the board at 1280 | Minor | **Logged.** Panel is resizable to 400 px; request sent to DesktopFoundation for a `defaultWidth` (or 400 below 1440). |
| 9 | Seven collapse buttons | Minor | **Logged**, kept (HubSpot pattern). |
| 10 | Seven "+ Add" rows on first use | Minor | **Logged**, kept (states.md "Tracker board"). |
