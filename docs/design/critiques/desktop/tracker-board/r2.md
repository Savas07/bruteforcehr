## Design Critique: Tracker board (desktop), round 2

### Overall Impression

The drag no longer moves the window, the Closed zones overlay the board without reflow, and keyboard steps work from either card control. One drag-feedback issue remains: the reason a zone is refused is hidden under the dragged card.

### Usability

| Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Severity    | Recommendation                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hovering a refused zone (Accepted / Declined before an offer) shows its reason inside that zone, exactly where the drag overlay card sits, so the reason is covered. The user sees a dimmed zone without knowing why. | 🟡 Moderate | Show the refused zone's reason in a fixed line under "Close as", above the zones; reserve its height so the zones don't jump. |
| ⇧⌘→ from the `M` menu trigger now moves the card, keeps focus on it in its new column and shows "Polder Energy · moved to Applied · Undo ⌘Z".                                                                         | ✅          | —                                                                                                                             |

Matches tracker.md (≈ 3.5–4.5 columns at 1280–1440, more at 1920).                                                       |

### Visual Hierarchy

- **What draws the eye first:** title and column headers, then cards. Correct.
- **Reading flow:** unchanged from r1; the inline envelope glyph now reads as part of the company line instead of a smudge on the monogram.
- **Emphasis:** during drag, the hovered target is the strongest element (teal ring + transition text). Correct.

### Consistency

| Element    | Issue                                                                                                               | Recommendation |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Drop zones | Dashed outline for valid, solid teal for hovered, dimmed for refused: consistent with column wells.                 | —              |
| Dark mode  | Wells on `surface.sunken`, cards on `surface.base`; timeline pills keep their phase tones; Not selected stays grey. | —              |

### Accessibility

- **Color contrast:** secondary text in dark wells is legible; axe run follows in the accessibility review.
- **Touch targets:** unchanged (≥ 24 px).
- **Text readability:** TR capture fits (Kaydedildi, Hazırlanıyor, Başvuruldu, "Bu aşamada 9 g"); summary line now Turkish.

### What Works Well

- The overlaid Closed zones keep every column still under the pointer.
- Moves land in board order with "New today", and counts, "oldest" lines and the Closed rail recount at once.
- Undo of the automatic Offer move tags the original row "Undone" and adds a "You" row with the reverse pills.

### Priority Recommendations

1. **Move the refused-zone reason out from under the drag card** — the user needs the "why" at the moment of refusal.
