## Design Critique: Tracker calendar

### Overall Impression
Answers "when" (grid) and "what's next" (agenda) side by side, as the research intended. Suggested follow-ups are visibly different (dashed outline + "Suggested" tag) from dates the user or an employer set, which keeps the trust rule. First pass: day cells named themselves differently from what they showed, and the agenda's open button was labelled with the company name.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| Grid cells carried `aria-label="Thursday, 22 October 2026, today: 2 items"` while showing "22" plus entry chips: axe `label-content-name-mismatch` ×9 per page. | 🟡 Moderate | Put the full date + count in screen-reader-only text inside the cell; let chips contribute their text; no `aria-label`. |
| Agenda entries' open button showed the company name ("Kervan Teknoloji") — reads like a tag, not an action. | 🟡 Moderate | Visible "Open", accessible "Open Kervan Teknoloji, Product Engineer" (label in name). |
| Month chips truncate at 1280 px ("16:00 Ki…"). The full text is in the tooltip and the agenda; type is the icon + tone, never colour alone. | 🟢 Minor | Accept; Week view shows up to 12 entries per day with more room. |
| Reminders can't be dragged to reschedule (research). | 🟢 Minor | Later; reschedule from the application detail for now. |
| No "Interviews without a time" tray: `Interview.startsAt` is required in the data model, so the case can't occur. | — | None. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: today's filled circle, then interview chips (accent tone).
- **Reading flow**: period heading "October 2026" (live region) → ‹ Today › → grid; agenda on the right lists the next 7 days by Timed / All day.
- **Emphasis**: deadlines use the warning tone with a flag icon, never red.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Deadlines | Same `collectDeadlines` as Today (reminders, assessment/offer reply, job closing). | — |
| Follow-ups | `@bfhr/core` `followUpDue` with the profile's `followUpAfterDays`; dismissed ones hidden, snoozed ones on their snooze date. | — |
| `.ics` | DesktopTracker's `interviewsToIcs` / `saveIcsFile` (RFC 5545, organizer zone). | — |
| Detail | Opening an entry shows the shared `ApplicationPanel` with "n of N on {day}". | — |

### Accessibility
- **axe**: after fixes, 0 critical · 0 serious · 0 moderate in every state × theme × engine.
- **Keyboard**: WAI-ARIA date grid — one tab stop; ←/→ day, ↑/↓ week, Home/End week ends, PageUp/PageDown month (week in Week view), T today, Enter/Space shows the day in the agenda; hidden weekends are stepped over.
- **Semantics**: `role="grid"` named by the period, `columnheader` weekdays, `aria-selected` on the chosen day.

### What Works Well
- Show menu persists types, suggestions and weekends per device.
- "Add to calendar (.ics)" on each interview plus "Upcoming interviews (.ics)" under Mod+E.

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Cell names from content.**
2. **"Open" as the agenda action label.**

## Resolution

| # | Finding | Severity | Status |

| 2 | Agenda open button labelled with company | Moderate | Fixed: visible "Open" (`tracker-calendar.openShort`), accessible name keeps company + role. |
| 3 | Chip truncation in Month | Minor | Accepted (tooltip, agenda, Week view). |
| 4 | Drag to reschedule reminders | Minor | Logged. |
