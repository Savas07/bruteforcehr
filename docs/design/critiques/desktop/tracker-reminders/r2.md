## Design Critique: Tracker reminders (desktop), round 2

### Overall Impression
The r1 fixes land without changing what the user sees, apart from two things: the denied switch is now usable, and a picked day shows 09:00. Focus now follows every step: snooze lands on **Show now** (`rem-snoozed`: the button carries the focus ring), Show now lands on **I followed up**, answers land on the page title, the editor and Remove land on Change / Set a reminder, and "Pick a date…" opens with the date field focused in both engines.

### Usability (new in round 2)

| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| After an answer, focus lands on the page title, which (being `tabIndex=-1`) shows no ring; the toast is the only visible confirmation of where things went. | 🟢 Minor | Log: same pattern as Today ("with nothing left, to the screen title"); the next Tab continues from the top of the page content, the screen reader reads the title. |
| Picking a day keeps a time the user typed earlier, and clearing the day keeps the time. | 🟢 Minor | Log: a time without a day is ignored on save (`reminderPatch`); the field stays as typed. |

No new Critical or Moderate.

### What Works Well
- One step per keystroke: `H` → `2` snoozes and leaves focus on the way back (Show now); `D` answers and leaves focus at the top.
- The denied state now matches its own instruction ("then switch this on again").
