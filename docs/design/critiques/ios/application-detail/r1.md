## Design Critique: Application detail, M3 additions (iOS) — r1

Scope: what M3 adds to the detail reviewed in tracker-board r1–r4: Reminder, the follow-up suggestion, interview rounds with their editor and prep reminder, Notes, Contacts (editor), Documents, Delete, the reminder / contact / notes editors and the notification permission explainer. Captures: `artifacts/screens/ios/tracker-audit/tracker-board-detail-m3{,-ax}`, `-reminder`, `-interview`, `-contact`, `-notes`, `-explainer` (each default and `-ax`, plus every scrolled audit page); live: `artifacts/screens/ios/tracker-live/tracker-e2e-{detail-new,detail-filled,explainer}.png`.

### Overall Impression
The detail now holds everything about one application without becoming a form: read-only rows that open a focused editor sheet, and ghost rows ("Set a reminder", "Add interview", "Add contact") where something can be added. The permission explainer previews the user's own reminder and asks once.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| Editor pickers (Type, Length, Their time zone, Prep reminder) clipped their values at AX5, first beside the label, then inside a `Menu` label | 🔴 Critical | Navigation row at accessibility sizes (label above value, pushes the option list with ✓) |
| Reminder sheet and explainer opened at the medium detent at AX5, cutting the form | 🟡 Moderate | Large detent at accessibility sizes |
| Remove / Delete rows in system red only nearly passed contrast and didn't wrap | 🟡 Moderate | `TrackerDestructiveRow`: danger-tone text, wraps, ≥ 44 pt |
| Follow-up card's primary action was "Draft follow-up", but compose doesn't exist until M5 | 🟡 Moderate | "Remind me to follow up" opens the reminder editor, pre-filled |
| A new reminder was pre-filled with the follow-up text even when not coming from the card (found by the live check) | 🟡 Moderate | Blank unless it comes from the suggestion |
| When the reminder matches the next interview, the header's Next step and the Reminder row say the same thing (fixture data) | 🟢 Minor | Acceptable: the user set both |
| Delete has no Undo | 🟢 Minor | A confirmation names what goes ("Its history, interviews and notes…"); the server deletes the events |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: role, company, status pill, then the one next step. Unchanged and correct.
- **Reading flow**: Reminder is right under the header (what to do next), then progress, suggestion, interviews, activity, then notes, contacts, documents and details. That goes from what to do now down to reference.
- **Emphasis**: "Overdue" in amber with the word; nothing red except Delete.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Section titles | Same `DetailSectionTitle` for every new section | — |
| Editors | One sheet pattern (Cancel / Save, grouped form, labels above values) for reminder, interview, contact, notes | — |
| Toasts | "{company} · reminder set", "· interview added", "· removed from this application"; removals offer Undo | — |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: passes; footers and remove rows fixed.
- **Touch targets**: ≥ 44 pt everywhere, including the Insights chip ✕ (was 17 pt).
- **Text readability**: verified at AX5 in screenshots; the audit exemptions and their evidence are in tracker-board/accessibility.md (M3).

### What Works Well
- The explainer asks only at the moment of need and shows exactly what will arrive (Tempo / Atoms pattern; D33).
- Prep reminders are a per-round choice on the device, and the row shows "Reminder 1 hour before".

### Priority Recommendations
1. **AX pickers** — done.
2. **Detents at AX** — done.
3. **Destructive rows** — done.

## Resolution
- `TrackerPickerRow`, `TrackerDestructiveRow`, large detents at accessibility sizes, follow-up card → reminder, blank new reminder, chip ✕ 44 pt.
- `testDetailEditorsReminder/Interview/Contact/Notes`, `testNotificationExplainer` and `testDetailM3` pass at both sizes; the live check passes end to end.
- Open: 0 Critical, 0 Moderate.
