## Design Critique: Preview

### Overall Impression
The page is the real cv-render HTML scaled to the screen: what you see is what the PDF prints, and it reads as a document, not app chrome. The biggest problem was at AX5, where the bottom bar and page pill covered most of the page.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| At AX5 the Share/Export pair stacks at huge sizes and the pill grows, covering ~45 % of the page | 🟡 Moderate | Put the page-count line above the page instead of over it; keep full Dynamic Type on the bar (a cap fails the audit) |
| The page-count pill sat over the name at the top of the page (first build) | 🟡 Moderate | Move it to the bottom of the page, above the bar |
| cv-render's own `width=device-width` viewport made the sheet render 1:1 and clip on the right (first build) | 🔴 Critical | Replace the viewport with the paper width so WebKit fits the sheet |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: the name on the page, then the primary "Share PDF" — correct.
- **Reading flow**: segment bar → page → Export / Share.
- **Emphasis**: one filled button (Share PDF); Export… secondary.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Bottom bar | Same ButtonPair + bar material as other sticky bars | — |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: chrome passes; the page is print in paper colours (exempt in the audit as printed document).
- **Touch targets**: chrome ≥ 44 pt. The page's links (e-mail, URLs) are print text, don't navigate in the preview, and are exempt.
- **Text readability**: pinch-to-zoom up to 4×.

### What Works Well
- One renderer: the same HTML is the PDF; page breaks are the PDF's (pill counts pages from the paged PDF).
- Re-renders keep the scroll position.

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Fit the sheet** (done). 2. **Clear the page at AX sizes** (done). 3. **Page pill off the name** (done).

## Resolution
- `CVWebPage.withViewport` replaces cv-render's viewport with `width=<paper px>`.
- The pill first moved to the bottom of the page; capping the bar and pill type size then failed the Dynamic Type audit, so the cap was removed and the page-count line now sits above the page (never over it) at every size.
- Audit: page text/links exempt as printed page (stated in the test); chrome audited at default and AX5 — 0 open issues.
