## Design Critique: Insights (iOS)

### Overall Impression
Reads as a calm summary: 2 × 2 tiles, a sentence-titled funnel with horizontal bars, then the breakdown, first reply and 12-week activity. Numbers always sit next to their n.

### Usability
|Finding|Severity|Recommendation|
|---|---|---|
|Range card: picker and date line were two rows with a large gap; big gaps between sections.|🟡 Moderate|**Fixed**: one row (picker + date), `.listSectionSpacing(.compact)`.|
|About sheet at the medium detent was glass over the screen: text contrast failed in the audit.|🟡 Moderate|**Fixed**: opaque grouped `presentationBackground`.|
|Section headers in system secondary grey "nearly passed" contrast.|🟡 Moderate|**Fixed**: `Fg.secondary` token.|
|Breakdown shows "Too few to compare" on most sources with fixture data.|🟢 Minor|By design (D31 n < 5); rows still show "Responses: 2 of 4" and drill into Tracker.|
|Long-press value callout on chart bars (research) not built.|🟢 Minor|Values print above every bar and VoiceOver reads them, so the callout adds nothing; dropped.|

### Visual Hierarchy
- First: large title, range, the four tiles. Then sentence "12 of 20 got a response". Correct order.
- One brand hue (`Chart.series`), grey track/zero bars; no status colours.

### Consistency
Shared `PhaseContainer`, `PaneError`, `EmptyState`, `ConnectionBannerSection`, tokens only; KPI tiles mirror desktop's set minus offer (iOS: Applications · Response rate · Interviews · First response, per research).

### Accessibility
Tiles, funnel stages, breakdown rows, week bars are buttons: label = name ("Applied", "Week of 12 October 2026"), value = numbers, hint "Shows these applications in Tracker". Tiles stack to one column and funnel bars move under their labels at AX sizes.

### What Works Well
- Drill-down pops to the Pipeline filtered to exactly the counted ids ("From Insights: …" chip), verified by the UI test.
- Week start follows the locale (Sunday in en_US, Monday in TR/DE).

### Priority Recommendations
1. Opaque about sheet. 2. Compact range row. 3. Token header colour.

## Resolution
All Moderate findings fixed; zero Critical, zero open Moderate. One finding belongs to IosTracker (Pipeline chip clear button 17 pt hit area) — reported to `agent://IosTracker`.
