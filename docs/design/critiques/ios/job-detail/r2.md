## Design Critique: Job detail — default size after r1, and AX5

### Overall Impression
At the default size the r1 fixes hold: the gap row now reads "Not in your CV yet: Vue.js" with
two clean buttons, and the skills read "React, TypeScript … Vue.js" in the posting's spelling. At
AX5 the screen was not usable: the sticky Apply bar grew to half the screen, leaving a sliver of
content, and several fixed-width layouts broke words apart.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| AX5: the Apply bar grows with the text ("Apply / on / com- / pany / site" over 5 lines plus a 100 pt bookmark) and covers ~45 % of an iPhone 17e screen; the content above it shows two lines at a time, and drags that start on the bar don't scroll | 🔴 Critical | Stop the sticky bar's text at AX1 (as the system bars do) and give its buttons the Large Content Viewer, so the label wraps to two lines and the content keeps the screen |
| AX5: the band legend under the bar breaks words ("StretPar- ch tial Goo d Stro ng") | 🟡 Moderate | The bar is decorative and the band is named above it at full size: cap the legend's text at xLarge; ask IosFoundation to switch the shared bar to a current-band-only label at AX sizes |
| AX5: the company monogram (scaled to ~90 pt) squeezes the company name into "Light- / house / Learning" | 🟡 Moderate | At AX sizes show the company name alone; the monogram is decorative |
| AX5: status glyphs keep a ~70 pt leading column in every reason / gap row, so text wraps at 3–4 words per line | 🟡 Moderate | At AX sizes put the glyph above the text (full-width text) |
| AX5: decorative icons in the counts ("checklist", "star", "circle.dashed") cause "Require- / ments: 2 of 3 / met" | 🟢 Minor | Drop the icons at AX sizes |
| AX5: impact tags wrap to two lines inside a capsule, which turns into an oval | 🟢 Minor | Rounded-rectangle tags |
| AX5: the reason sheet's medium detent shows only the title and subtitle; the chips are below the fold | 🟡 Moderate | Large detent only at AX sizes |
| Open original posting sat inside the More menu; the brief asks for share / open-original in the toolbar | 🟡 Moderate | Make Open original a toolbar button next to Share (Safari symbol, "Opens in your browser" hint); More keeps Not for me / Show again / Tell us why, and disappears when it would be empty |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first** (AX5): the company name and job title, then the band label — same order as at the default size, which is right.
- **Reading flow**: unchanged; at AX5 each section becomes a single column (fact rows already stacked label over value).
- **Emphasis**: the band label stays the largest text in the match card at AX5; "84 of 100" stays secondary.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| TR button copy | Several buttons used the siz imperative ("Orijinal ilanı açın", "E-postayla başvurun") while the shared buttons use the bare form ("Geri al", "Daha az göster"); sentences use siz | Bare form for buttons, siz for sentences (as the shared catalog does) |
| Capsule badges | Fixed in r1; the shared `.bfhrCompact` style has landed, so the private workaround goes | Use `.labelStyle(.bfhrCompact)` |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: unchanged from r1 (verified in the accessibility pass).
- **Touch targets**: at AX1 the Apply and bookmark buttons stay ≥ 44 pt; the Large Content Viewer covers the capped labels.
- **Text readability**: AX5 body text is never truncated; words only break where the layout had no room (fixed above).

### What Works Well
- The gap buttons sit side by side at the default size and stack in German / at AX sizes.
- Fact rows already switch to label-over-value at AX sizes.
- The source rows, evidence lines and "Not stated" values keep their meaning at AX5.

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Cap the sticky Apply bar at AX1 + Large Content Viewer** — without it the screen can't be read at AX5.
2. **Full-width text at AX sizes** — glyph above text, no decorative icons, name without monogram.
3. **Open original in the toolbar** — one tap to the source the user trusts most.

## Resolution

| Finding | Severity | Resolution |
|---------|----------|------------|
| AX5 Apply bar covers the screen | 🔴 Critical | **Fixed** (commit "feat(ios): job detail AX: capped Apply bar…"): `.dynamicTypeSize(...accessibility1)` on the bar, `accessibilityShowsLargeContentViewer` on Save and Apply |
| Band legend breaks words | 🟡 Moderate | **Fixed** (commit "feat(ios): job detail AX layouts…") with a local `.dynamicTypeSize(...xLarge)` cap; shared fix requested from IosFoundation |
| Monogram squeezes the name | 🟡 Moderate | **Fixed** (capped-bar commit): name only at AX sizes |
| Glyph column at AX5 | 🟡 Moderate | **Fixed** (AX layouts commit): `GlyphLayout` stacks glyph above text for reasons, gaps and the hidden banner |
| Count icons at AX5 | 🟢 Minor | **Fixed** (AX layouts commit) |
| Oval tags | 🟢 Minor | **Fixed** (AX layouts commit): `BFHRRadius.sm` rounded rectangles |
| Reason sheet detent at AX5 | 🟡 Moderate | **Fixed** (AX layouts commit): `[.large]` at AX sizes |
| Open original in the More menu | 🟡 Moderate | **Fixed** (AX layouts commit): toolbar button next to Share |
| TR button forms | 🟢 Minor | **Fixed** (AX layouts commit) |
| Private CompactLabelStyle | 🟢 Minor | **Fixed** (capped-bar commit): `.bfhrCompact` from main |
