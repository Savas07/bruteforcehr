## Design Critique: Job preferences

### Overall Impression

Clear and quiet: six rows, each naming what's set, and each opening the exact editor the user saw during onboarding. Edits save at once like every other setting. Round 1 found one moderate problem on the error state and two consistency issues (a repeated title, place names in the wrong language).

### Usability

| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The page's failed state reused Settings' "We couldn't load your settings. Changes you make still save." — on this page nothing can be changed without data, and there was no way to retry. | 🟡 Moderate | `InlineAlert(.warning)` "We couldn't load your preferences." with **Try again** (`bfhrReload`). |
| A section page repeated its navigation title as the section header ("Where" above "Where"); the audit also flagged the header's contrast under the scroll edge. | 🟢 Minor | No section title on section pages (`bfhrPreferenceHeaderTitle`); counters and tags stay. |
| Typing into a field (role, place) left the keyboard over the chips below. | 🟢 Minor | Scrolling dismisses the keyboard; the number pad has Done (shared with onboarding r1). |
| Every change writes at once; an unchanged draft writes nothing (tested), and opening a page writes nothing. | — | Keep. |

### Visual Hierarchy

- **What draws the eye first**: the row titles (Roles, Seniority, Where, Salary floor, Work authorization, Languages), then their values in secondary text — correct for a summary.
- **Reading flow**: top to bottom in the onboarding order; the footer explains the effect ("Changes count from the next time your searches run").
- **Emphasis**: "Not set" is secondary text, not a warning — nothing here is required.

### Consistency

| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Place names | Summaries showed the stored label, in the language the place was picked in (a German user saw "Germany"). | Country-only places are named from their code in the app's language (`JobPreferencesDraft.displayName`); cities keep their label. 🟢 Minor |
| Settings row | First row of Settings in its own group (IA.md "Account · Job preferences"), accent tile with the briefcase, first role as the trailing value, like the other rows' values. | — |
| Copy | Same strings as onboarding (table `Onboarding`), EN / TR siz / DE du. | — |

### Accessibility

See `accessibility.md`.

### What Works Well

- Title over value rows never truncate the role list, at any text size.
- The editors are the onboarding sections, so a change made here looks exactly like the choice made on day one.
- The deep link opens a single section (`bruteforcehr://settings/job-preferences/salary`).

### Priority Recommendations

1. **Error state with Try again.**
2. **Place names in the app's language.**
3. **No repeated section title.**

## Resolution

| # | Finding | Severity | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Misleading error, no retry | Moderate | Fixed: `jobPreferences.loadFailed` + Try again (`jobPreferences.loadFailed` identifier). |
| 2 | Repeated title | Minor | Fixed: `.environment(\.bfhrPreferenceHeaderTitle, false)` on section pages. |
| 3 | Keyboard over chips | Minor | Fixed: `.scrollDismissesKeyboard(.immediately)`; number pad Done. |
| 4 | Place names in the picking language | Minor | Fixed: `JobPreferencesDraft.displayName(of:locale:)` in both summaries and the place tokens (tested). |
