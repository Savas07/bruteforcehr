## Design Critique: Mail review, live

### Overall Impression
The queue keeps its calm, approved shape, and the new pieces slot in without new chrome. Two of them sit where people won't see them at the sheet's medium detent: the suggested interview time and the email sheet's Add interview.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| The review sheet shows the suggested interview after the email and the fields, below the medium detent. The time is what most invites are about, and Confirm adds it to the calendar without the user having seen it | 🟡 Moderate | Put the interview card right under the proposal and reason, before the email |
| The read-only email of an automatically applied invite has Add interview at the end of the scroll, below the medium detent | 🟡 Moderate | Pin Add interview at the bottom like the review sheet's Confirm / Change… |
| Updates footer says "Undo puts the email back in To review", but Undo of an automatic move now reverts the move and files the email as corrected (ADR 0002, the trigger) | 🟡 Moderate | "Changes we made from your email in the last 14 days. Undo moves the application back." |
| Change…: picking a status by hand would double-move once the trigger applies the category's target | 🟡 Moderate | The status row is derived from category + application (`reviewMoveTarget`), with the footer "The status change follows the category." |
| Undo of Confirm can't go back to `pending` once written (rules) | 🟡 Moderate | Hold the review write for the toast window (10.5 s) and flush on background; Undo drops the held write (verified live, see r1 live check) |
| The interview card says "The email didn't name a time zone, so this uses yours" in amber when the zone is missing | 🟢 Minor | Kept: it's the honest state, with symbol + text |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: the proposal sentence ("Move Nordlicht Mobility … from Acknowledged to Screening"), then Confirm. Correct.
- **Reading flow**: proposal → reason → interview (after the fix) → email → fields → Not job mail.
- **Emphasis**: Add interview on Updates rows is a plain text button under the change, quieter than Undo's position; right for an optional follow-up.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Email sheet vs. review sheet | Different action placement (scrolling vs. pinned) | Pin Add interview (above) |
| Timeline email rows | "Email received" with no sender | "Email from Lena Vogel" + Open email |
| Scanning card | Same copy pattern as Today's card | Kept, the two share wording |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: suggestion card (sunken surface), amber zone note and the toast pass in both themes (tokens; audited in `MailReviewAccessibilityAuditTests.testLivePanes`).
- **Touch targets**: Add interview and Open email are 44 pt plain buttons; the pinned button is full width.
- **Text readability**: the card combines into one VoiceOver element: "Interview in this email, Tue 28 Oct, 15:00, 10:00 in Eastern Time, 45 min, meet.google.com".

### What Works Well
- Nothing is created automatically: the interview waits for Confirm or Add interview, as ADR 0012 requires.
- The organiser's local time appears next to the user's whenever the zones differ.
- Every decision keeps a toast Undo, and automatic changes keep their durable Undo on Updates and the timeline.

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Show the interview before the email** in the review sheet.
2. **Pin Add interview** in the email sheet.
3. **Fix the Updates footer** to say what Undo does now.

## Resolution
- `ReviewSheet`: `InterviewSuggestionCard` right after the proposal block, before the evidence.
- `MailEmailSheet`: Add interview in a bottom `safeAreaInset` bar (`.bar` background), shown only while the interview isn't on the calendar.
- `mail.updates.footer` → "… Undo moves the application back." (EN/TR/DE); `mail.change.footer` explains the derived status.
- Review writes held for `FirestoreUserDataStore.reviewHold`, flushed on background; Undo drops them.
- Timeline: `TimelineEntry.email` → "Email from {sender}" + Open email (`bruteforcehr://mail/review/{id}` opens the email or its review).
