## Design Critique: Mail tab, review sheet, email sheet, timeline email

### Overall Impression

Review is now server-authoritative and reads that way: Confirm / Change… / Not job mail say what will happen, the toast's Undo drops the held decision, automatic moves keep their source and Undo, and the email sheet offers Add interview only while the invite isn't on the calendar. The live run confirmed the data on a real project (below).

### Visual Hierarchy

- **What draws the eye first**: To review (3) and the amber Reconnect card when an inbox needs it.
- **Reading flow**: scope + last check → chips → attention card → "We weren't sure about these" rows.
- **Emphasis**: at AX5 the chips stack, the attention card scrolls with the list, the tab bar stays system size.

### What Works Well

- Every automatic change keeps source, reason and Undo.
- Confirm is disabled, not hidden, without an application; the reason is in the sheet.
- One tap from the timeline to the email that caused the change.
