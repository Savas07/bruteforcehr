# Mail review (iOS): critique round 3 (final)

Context: Mail tab after rounds 1–2; user goal "check what the app wasn't sure about and confirm or correct it in seconds"; persona: active job seeker, often stressed and short on time, switching between phone and desktop. Stage: refinement. Focus: the full capture matrix (iPhone 17e and iPhone 17 Pro Max × populated / empty / loading / error / offline × light / dark × default / AX5) plus the interaction captures (queue scrolled, review sheet, Change…, Updates, All job mail; default and AX5), and the XCUITest accessibility audit.

## Design Critique: Mail review — final round

### Overall Impression
The tab now does its one job calmly: a visible scope and freshness line, chips with the To review count (the only tab badge), a single-sentence Reconnect card, and review rows that each carry evidence, the exact proposal and its reason. The sheet is opaque, pins Change… / Confirm, and Confirm only ever does what the sentence says. Remaining issues are cosmetic or belong to shared components.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| At AX5 the Reconnect card's address hyphenates ("deniz.ak- / soy@ex- / ample.net") because `InlineAlert` keeps its glyph column. Meaning is intact; the card is ~1 screen tall at AX5. | 🟢 Minor | Shared component: requested from IosFoundation (glyph above the title at accessibility sizes). |
| The skeleton redacts the chips into shapes instead of showing their labels. | 🟢 Minor | Logged: `PhaseContainer` redacts the whole skeleton by design; the chips' shapes and positions match the loaded layout, and the error state shows real chips. |
| Row time shows "3:18 AM" in the simulator's time zone while the fixture clock is UTC. | 🟢 Minor | Logged: correct behaviour (local time); captures use the simulator zone. |
| In Updates, long sender addresses wrap the reason to four lines on the smallest phone. | 🟢 Minor | Logged: the reason is the trust evidence (§2.6); it must not truncate. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: the selected chip, then each row's bold proposal ("Move Tannenbaum Software · … from Applied to Acknowledged"). Correct for a decision queue.
- **Reading flow**: scope + freshness → chips → health card → "We weren't sure about these" → rows → footer ("Nothing changes until you confirm…"). The footer answers the stressed user's "what happens if I tap?" before they act.
- **Emphasis**: amber is used only for the broken account, "Needs a look" and "To review" state lines; nothing is red; "Not selected" is never shown as a failure.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Light / dark | Tokens hold in both; amber card and teal chip keep contrast in dark | Keep |
| Devices | Pro Max shows more of each row on one line; nothing reflows oddly | Keep |
| Shared components | AutoChangeRow, ReviewSheet, StatusMenu, InlineAlert, PaneError, EmptyState used as-is | Keep |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: audit passes after round 3 (dot removed, section headers on `Fg.secondary`, opaque review sheet). Remaining reported contrast items are content under the floating tab bar's scroll-edge blur and system glass toolbar buttons, documented as exclusions in `accessibility.md`.
- **Touch targets**: chips, scope menu, Undo, sheet buttons ≥ 44 pt.
- **Text readability**: at AX5 rows stack monogram / sender / time and drop line limits; nothing that carries meaning truncates.

### What Works Well
- No "Confirm all" anywhere; swipe Confirm is disabled when an email has no application, so a blind swipe can't move the wrong application.
- Every automatic change in Updates names its source and reason and has Undo (or "Can't undo" when superseded).
- Empty (no account), error and offline states each say what happened and the one next step, in a calm voice.

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Shared InlineAlert AX layout** — requested from IosFoundation.
2. Nothing else blocks the design checkpoint.
