## Design Critique: Onboarding

### Overall Impression

Ready. Every r1 finding is fixed and visible in the captures; the live run completes the whole flow on Firebase in about two minutes of test time and writes exactly the agreed values. Nothing Critical or Moderate is open.

### Usability

| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| r1 #1–#4 (number pad, plural line, skills, empty search) | — | Verified: Done closes the number pad (live run checks the keyboard is gone and the EU answer is selected); "Found 3 roles and 3 skills" / DE "3 Rollen und 3 Fähigkeiten gefunden"; skills are the keywords (`swift`, `kotlin`, `sql`); `finish-nothing` shows the explanation with **Back to preferences** and **Go to Today**. |
| Step 1 in the error state still offers Continue (preferences then start empty). | — | Keep: research ("The user can still Continue"). |
| At AX5, Preferences is long (8 pages on iPhone 17e). | 🟢 Minor | Accepted: every section is optional and scrolling dismisses the keyboard; one question per screen was rejected in research for time (≈ 90 s more). |

### Visual Hierarchy

- Finishing keeps the primary button's colors with the spinner and "Setting up your search…", and Back / Skip dim while it runs.
- Finish's summary rows read title over value; long place lists wrap under their title at every size (iPhone 17 Pro Max AX5 checked).

### Consistency

| Element | Issue | Recommendation |

### Accessibility

See `accessibility.md`: all seven `OnboardingAccessibilityAuditTests` pass (every state, default and AX5, TR and DE, plus the behavior checks).

### What Works Well

- The CV is read in the background and saved to CV Studio; Preferences arrive pre-filled with "From CV" tags that go away once edited.
- Every step can be skipped, and the app resumes at the same step after a relaunch.
- Light and dark are identical in structure; tokens only.
