## Design Critique: Today (iOS), round 4

### Overall Impression
Today does its job in two seconds: what's next, what needs a decision, what changed on its own, what's due, and what's new, in that order and in calm, neutral type. Both devices and both themes hold up, and AX5 reads as a long but well-structured list. Nothing Critical or Moderate is left; a few taste-level notes remain.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| To review now splits into "To review · 3" (decisions) and "Updated automatically" (changes with source, reason and Undo). The count matches what's under it. | — | Keep. |
| The ReviewSheet opens with Confirm / Change… pinned at the bottom; the decision is visible without scrolling at both detents. | — | Keep (opens at large; medium is one drag away). |
| Pro Max shows ~1.5 sections more per screen; nothing stretches oddly. | — | Keep. |

| "This week in Insights" footer link is not shown. | 🟢 Minor | Intentional for M0: Insights has no screen yet, and `bruteforcehr://insights` only selects the Tracker tab, so the link would lead nowhere real. Add with the Insights screen. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: the large title, then the Next up card with its accent kind line. Correct on both devices and themes.
- **Reading flow**: fixed order, one concern per section; the "Updated automatically" header separates FYI from decisions.
- **Emphasis**: the only colored text is the accent (kind line, Undo, Draft follow-up, See all in Jobs). Deadlines and times are neutral. Nothing red anywhere.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Automatic changes | Today and Mail › Updates share `AutoChangeRow`. | Keep. |
| Monograms | Hidden at AX sizes everywhere (`hidesAtAccessibilitySizes`). | Keep. |
| Prep row | Custom chevron row instead of `DisclosureGroup`, to keep Dynamic Type intact; looks like the system disclosure. | Keep. |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: every text token pair on this screen is ≥ 5.78:1 (light and dark), see [accessibility.md](accessibility.md).
- **Touch targets**: ≥ 44 pt; at AX sizes match actions become labelled buttons.
- **Text readability**: no truncation of meaning at AX5 on either device; long company names wrap at word boundaries.

### What Works Well
- Trust: every automatic change names its source (email + sender + subject) and offers Undo inline, by swipe and in the context menu.
- Calm: "Nothing needs you yet" and "You're all caught up" without praise; follow-ups say "No reply in 9 days. Send a short follow-up?" instead of "overdue".
- One-gesture triage: leading swipe = Confirm / Looks right / Follow up / Done / Save; trailing = Snooze · Not job mail / Not needed / Not for me; context menu adds Review… / Open application / Open job.

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Keep the structure**; no further layout changes for M0.
2. **Pull to refresh with the real data source (M1).**
3. **Insights footer link once the Insights screen exists.**

## Resolution

Zero Critical, zero Moderate. The matrix was re-captured after the last accessibility fixes (Prep row as one button element); the screenshots above are that final capture. Minor items are logged above (fixture language, pull to refresh, Insights link) with the milestone that resolves them. Round closed.
