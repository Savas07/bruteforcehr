## Design Critique: Application detail + sheets

### Overall Impression
The detail does what the brief asks: the header names the role, company and location, the status pill opens Move to…, there's one next step ("Interview · In 6 days, 5:00 PM"), a progress rail, and a day-grouped timeline. In that timeline the automatic move reads "Moved to Interviewing · Email from marcus@brightwave.io: '…' · Automatic · Mail · View source · Undo". The r1 fixes hold: rows push, headers are opaque, the sheets name the application, and AX rows keep whole words. What's left is polish in the detail and one move path (drag) that doesn't start.

### Usability
| Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| Drag from a row never starts: the long press opens the context menu, and moving the finger then selects menu items. The XCUITest drag (0.5 / 0.8 / 1.2 s presses onto the strip's "Preparing" chip) never drops. One of the three required move paths is missing. | 🟡 Moderate | Attach `.draggable` to the row content (SwiftUI's own drag interaction) instead of the List row, and make the stage strip chips the drop targets (the floating drop panel only when the strip has scrolled away). Verify with `testDragOntoStageStrip`. |
| Detail at AX5: the 36 pt monogram scales to about 90 pt and hyphenates the role ("Front-end Engi-neer, Editor"), the same problem as the r1 rows. | 🟡 Moderate | Hide the decorative monogram at accessibility sizes. |
| Contact email is a 16 pt tall text link, and VoiceOver reads only the raw address. | 🟡 Moderate | A full-width 44 pt link with an envelope glyph, labelled "Email Marcus Reid", the address as the hint. |
| Details rows (`LabeledContent`) put values in the system's lighter grey, and they don't wrap: the audit flags "Applied · 17 days ago" as clipped at AX5. | 🟡 Moderate | Custom fact rows: label `Fg.primary`, value `Fg.secondary`, side by side and stacking when they don't fit. |
| The header doesn't say that the last move was automatic. You only see that in the timeline, two screens down. | 🟢 Minor | Under the pill: "✉ Last change came from an email". The timeline row keeps the reason, View source and Undo. |
| Next-step line built from three texts ("Interview", "·", date). The audit measures the lone "·" as low contrast, and at large sizes the parts wrap separately. | 🟢 Minor | One `Text` ("Interview · In 6 days, 5:00 PM") that wraps as a sentence. |

### Visual Hierarchy
- **What draws the eye first**: the role title in the header card, then the teal "Interviewing" pill. Correct.
- **Reading flow**: header → progress ("Interviewing, stage 6 of 7 · Next: Offer") → interviews → activity by day → details → contacts. The order matches the persona's questions: where am I, what's next, what happened, what did I send.
- **Emphasis**: system section headers ("Progress", "Interviews", "Details") are lighter than the day headers ("Mon, Oct 12"), so two levels of grouping fight. Use one header style.

### Consistency
| Element | Issue | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Section headers | System grey (≈ 4.4:1, the audit says "nearly passed") vs our `Fg.secondary` day headers (7:1). | One `DetailSectionTitle`: subheadline semibold, `Fg.secondary`. |
| Move sheet | iOS 26 title + subtitle now come from `navigationSubtitle` (r1 used a custom principal view that the audit flagged as clipped). | Keep the system subtitle. |
| Toasts | "Aire Digital Health · moved to Preparing" and "… · marked as not selected. Your notes stay here." follow the shared pattern; Undo → "Restored". | — |

### Accessibility
- **Color contrast**: token pairs used here are 4.6–17:1 in light and dark (accessibility.md table). Failures came from the system header grey and the lone "·" glyph (fixed above).
- **Touch targets**: email link fixed; chips, headers, sheet rows, toast Undo ≥ 44 pt.
- **Text readability**: AX5 header fixed; timeline rows (shared `TimelineRow`) wrap well.

### What Works Well
- The automatic change carries its source (Automatic · Mail), the evidence sentence, View source and Undo. The older manual moves show "You", and the one that can't be undone would say so.
- The progress rail ends at the current stage with "stage 6 of 7 · Next: Offer". Closed applications end at the grey outcome.
- The Close… sheet lists Not selected / Withdrawn / No response, with Accepted and Declined disabled and the reason shown. The system ✕ no longer competes with "Close…".

### Priority Recommendations
1. **Make drag work** (move path 3 of 3).
2. **AX5 header and fact rows**: the detail must read at the largest sizes.
3. **One header style and a proper contact target.**
