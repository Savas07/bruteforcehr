## Design Critique: Tracker after the drag decision

### Overall Impression
With drag removed, the Pipeline offers two clear and complete ways to move an application, and no gesture that looks like it should work but doesn't. Long press (or the VoiceOver action, the status pill, the detail's ⋯ menu) opens Move to…. Swipe right moves to the next stage; swipe left opens Move to… or Close…. Every status is reachable, every move goes through the state machine, and every move offers Undo.
