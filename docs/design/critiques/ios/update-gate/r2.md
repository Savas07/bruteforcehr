## Design Critique: Update gate (iOS), round 2

### Overall Impression

The r1 fixes work: at AX5 the banner is one calm line above the tab bar and the blocking screen reads top to bottom. Turkish and German fit at the default size on the 17e with room to spare. The audit run found two real defects on the blocking screen at AX5 (a truncated German sentence, text under the status bar) and a borderline contrast on the banner title.

### Accessibility

- **Color contrast**: see the table in r1; banner title now `fg-primary`.
- **Touch targets**: unchanged (≥ 44 pt).
- **Text readability**: German AX5 truncation (fixed below); everything else wraps.

### What Works Well

- AX5 behavior is now proportionate: the optional notice shrinks, the blocking explanation gets the whole screen.
- Translations keep the tone: no urgency words in any language.

### Priority Recommendations

1. **Fix the German truncation** — the explanation must never be cut.
2. **Status-bar strip on the blocking screen.**
3. **Banner title in the primary text color.**

## Resolution

1. `UpdateRequiredScreen`: each body text has its own `fixedSize`; a canvas strip covers the top safe area.
2. `UpdateBanner`: own view with the `InlineAlert` anatomy; title `fg-primary`, sentence `fg-primary`, action `accent-text`, glyph `arrow.down.app` in `info-text`, info-tinted background and border on an opaque elevated card.
3. Copy: TR "Yardım", DE "Hilfe".
4. Audit harness: the banner audit judges only the banner's own elements; on the blocking screen's scrolled AX pages the screenshot-based checks are logged instead of failing (XCUITest keeps the pre-scroll frames of the scroll view's texts, so they measure the wrong pixels — the same limitation `TodayAccessibilityAuditTests` documents); the scrolled pages are saved and reviewed by eye.
