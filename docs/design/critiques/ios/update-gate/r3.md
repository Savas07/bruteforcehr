## Design Critique: Update gate (iOS), round 3

### Overall Impression

Both surfaces are calm and complete in every language, size, theme and device. The banner is a quiet card above the tab bar that says what, which version and where, and collapses to one line at AX5; the blocking screen explains, reassures, shows the versions and offers one way forward plus help. Nothing Critical or Moderate is left.

### Visual Hierarchy

- **What draws the eye first**: banner — its bold title in the tinted card, after Today's own content; blocking screen — the title, then the teal primary.
- **Reading flow**: glyph → title → sentence → action (banner); glyph → title → message → reassurance → versions → Open TestFlight → Get help (blocking).
- **Emphasis**: the banner title in the primary text color reads more clearly than the blue title did, and the info tint still marks it as a notice.

### Accessibility

- **Color contrast**: every pair ≥ 4.5:1 (table in [accessibility.md](accessibility.md)); the audit's contrast check passes on the banner and on the blocking screen's first viewport everywhere.
- **Touch targets**: ≥ 44 pt for ✕, Open TestFlight, the one-line AX banner button, the primary and Get help.
- **Text readability**: full text at AX5 in all three languages on both devices (captures and scrolled audit pages reviewed).

### What Works Well

- Fail-open behavior means the gate can never lock someone out because of a network problem, and the copy never sounds alarming.
- One way forward on the blocking screen, with versions for support and help that isn't a dead end.
- The banner is app-wide without touching any screen, and toasts still stack above it.

### Priority Recommendations

1. **Keep the design**; no further changes for M1.
2. **Shared rule for floating cards**: elevation on the background shape only (follow-up for the components owner; the toast has the same pattern).
3. **Real TestFlight check after the first upload**: confirm `itms-beta://` lands on the app's build in TestFlight on a device.

## Resolution

Zero Critical, zero Moderate. Final changes in this round: German message "…um die App weiter zu nutzen." (the per-text `fixedSize` alone didn't stop the last-line truncation), and the banner's shadow drawn by its background shape only (the shadow on the whole view put a shadow under every text, which made the audit fail "Open TestFlight" on contrast on the 17e in light mode). The matrix was re-captured and re-audited after both; the audit run is recorded below. Minor items are accepted above with their reasons.

Final audit run, 2026-10-23 (`UpdateGateAccessibilityAuditTests`, 10 tests: banner and blocking screen at the default size and AX5, TR/DE at both sizes, banner closes and shows on every tab, blocking screen can't be dismissed, translation captures): **passed** on iPhone 17e light, iPhone 17e dark, iPhone 17 Pro Max light and iPhone 17 Pro Max dark. Logged, not failing: element-less issues, Today rows behind the banner, scrolled-page visual checks, and the German clipping prediction ([accessibility.md](accessibility.md), "Known false positive").
