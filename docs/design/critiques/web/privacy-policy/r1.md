## Design Critique: Privacy Policy (`/privacy/`)

### Overall Impression

Calm, legible and trustworthy: a big plain title, an effective date, a numbered contents list, and a teal "In short" summary at the top of every section that answers the anxious question before the legal text. The biggest problem is the service-provider table, which collapses into an unreadable four-column grid at 375 px — exactly where a person arriving from the Gmail consent screen on a phone needs it.

### Visual Hierarchy

- **What draws the eye first**: the "Privacy Policy" display title, then the teal "In short" callouts — correct; the summaries are the trust device.
- **Reading flow**: title → one-line description → effective date → contents → intro → numbered sections. At 1440 the sticky contents column on the left keeps orientation; the body column is ~68 characters wide.
- **Emphasis**: section numbers in tertiary grey keep the headings quiet; scope names in mono `code` (`gmail.readonly`, `Mail.Send`) stand out as precise technical facts, which suits the Google reviewer without shouting at the user.

### Consistency

| Tables vs lists | Section 7 table vs section 8 table: same component, but section 7's content is not really tabular.               | Fixed with the Usability finding above (service list). |

### Accessibility

- **Color contrast**: body `fg.primary` on `canvas` 16.26:1 (light) / 16.39:1 (dark); `fg.secondary` lead 7.31 / 8.63; `fg.tertiary` numbers 5.46 / 6.47; "In short" label `accent.text` on `surface.selected` 6.10 / 6.27; links `fg.link` 6.53 / 8.40 and underlined. Pass.
- **Touch targets**: contents links are ≥ 44 px tall; header items `min-h-11` (44 px). Inline links in prose are exempt (inline, SC 2.5.8 exception).
- **Text readability**: 16–17 px body, 26–28 px line height, ≤ 68 ch line length. Hyphenation and the 375 table are the readability issues above.

### What Works Well

- The "In short" summaries answer the stressful question first ("Other email is never stored", "Nothing is sent … until you've reviewed that message") — plain second-person sentences, no legalese.
- Mail handling is subdivided per provider with the exact scopes and the separate send permission, which both reassures the user and satisfies Google's disclosure requirement.
- Effective date is visible without scrolling; numbered sections are citeable in support replies.
- Dark mode keeps the summary callout distinct (`#0D302D`) without glare.

### Priority Recommendations

1. **Make section 7 readable on phones** — replace the 4-column table with stacked per-service blocks.
2. **Stop hyphenating English** — scope `hyphens: auto` to `de` / `tr`.
3. **Fix the contents list** — first-line number alignment, two columns at tablet widths — and the footer spacing.
