## Design Critique: Support (`/support/`)

### Overall Impression

Clear and calm: one primary action ("Open a GitHub issue") with an honest expectation ("run by one person, so a reply can take a few days"), a visible privacy warning before anyone posts publicly, and fourteen answers grouped by the moments people get stuck. The main defects are text joins: several answers render words glued together ("select More info, thenRun anyway"), plus the shared hyphenation and footer-spacing issues.

### Visual Hierarchy

- **What draws the eye first**: the "Support" title, then the filled "Open a GitHub issue" button — correct, it's the single primary action (`accent.solid` used once on the page).
- **Reading flow**: title → lead → primary card (description → amber "Issues are public" note → button) → two secondary cards → "Common questions" → topic chips → grouped questions. At 1440 the primary card and the two secondary cards sit side by side (3 : 2), and the FAQ uses a group-title column on the left.
- **Emphasis**: the amber privacy note is the right level — noticeable, not alarming (warning tone, not danger).

### Consistency

| Element        | Issue                                                                                                                                                 | Recommendation |

| Cards          | Primary and secondary cards share radius `lg`, `line.subtle` border, `surface.base`; secondary cards are whole-card links with hover `surface.hover`. | —              |

| Icons          | Arrow-up-right marks links that leave for GitHub; arrow-right for on-site; decorative, `aria-hidden`.                                                 | —              |

### Accessibility

- **Color contrast**: body `fg.secondary` on `surface.base` 7.84:1 (light); amber note `warning.text` on `warning.subtle` 4.61:1 (light) / 5.16:1 (dark); button `fg.onAccent` on `accent.solid` 6.51:1 / 4.58:1; topic-chip borders `line.control` on `canvas` 3.51:1 / 4.28:1 (non-text, ≥ 3:1). Pass.
- **Touch targets**: button, chips and question rows `min-h-11` (44 px); secondary cards are entirely clickable.
- **Text readability**: 16–17 px answers, ≤ 68 ch measure.

### What Works Well

- Support without an email address is explained, not hidden: GitHub account needed, public issues, reply expectations.
- The "Issues are public" warning sits _above_ the button, so it's read before the click.
- The FAQ covers the real first-run blockers of this product: SmartScreen on the unsigned Windows build, TestFlight invite-only, the Google "unverified app" screen, missing sign-in codes.
- Questions use native `<details>`: keyboard and screen-reader friendly with no JS.

### Priority Recommendations

1. **Fix the glued words in answers** — they break the exact instructions people follow.
2. **Fix the footer spacing.**
3. **Stop hyphenating English.**
