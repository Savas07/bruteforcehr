## Design Critique: Terms of Use (`/terms/`)

### Overall Impression

Short, plain and honest: twelve numbered sections, each opening with a one-line "In short" summary, and no wall of capitals. It reads like a person explaining the rules. The layout issues are the shared legal-layout ones (hyphenated English, a narrow single-column contents card at 768) plus the broken footer spacing.

### Usability

| Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Severity    | Recommendation                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| English body text is auto-hyphenated ("ap-plications" at 768, several per screen at 375). Slows skimming of a page people mostly skim.                                                                                                                                                    | 🟡 Moderate | Restrict `hyphens: auto` to `:lang(de)` / `:lang(tr)`.                                                                                                              |
| At 768 the contents card is a single 12-row column over the full width; the first section starts below the first screen.                                                                                                                                                                  | 🟡 Moderate | Two-column contents between 768 and 1279 px.                                                                                                                        |
| Footer text runs together: "not working?Get help…", "© 2026BruteForceHR".                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | 🟡 Moderate | Explicit spaces in the footer markup.                                                                                                                               |
| The most consequential section for this product — "Jobs from other websites" (scraping risk) — is section 6 of 12 and its summary is the only one that says "at your own risk". It's findable in the contents, but a user skimming summaries could miss that sources can be switched off. | 🟢 Minor    | Keep the order (account → sending → content → mail → sources is the order people meet them in the app); the summary already states the risk. Log for the copy pass. |
| No governing-law / jurisdiction section.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | 🟢 Minor    | Not a layout issue; needs the owner's decision (listed in the report). Log.                                                                                         |

### Visual Hierarchy

- **What draws the eye first**: the "Terms of Use" title, then the teal summaries — correct.
- **Reading flow**: title → description ("It's a free personal tool, provided as is.") → effective date → contents → intro → sections. Clear.
- **Emphasis**: bulleted rules in "Your account", "You're in control of what you send" and "Fair use" break up the text well; no section is visually louder than another, which suits terms.

### Consistency

| Element | Issue                                                                                                                                  | Recommendation |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| Layout  | Same `LegalLayout` as the Privacy Policy: title, effective-date pill, contents, numbered `h2`s, summaries.                             | —              |
| Wording | "Settings › Data & privacy › Delete account" and "Settings › Mail accounts" match the IA's Settings page names and the Privacy Policy. | —              |
| Links   | Cross-link to the Privacy Policy in the intro and in "Your content"; both underlined `fg.link`.                                        | —              |

### Accessibility

- **Color contrast**: same token pairs as the Privacy Policy: body 16.26:1 / 16.39:1, summaries' label 6.10:1 / 6.27:1, links 6.53:1 / 8.40:1. Pass.
- **Touch targets**: contents links ≥ 44 px; header and footer links `min-h-11`.
- **Text readability**: 16–17 px body, ≤ 68 ch measure; hyphenation is the only issue.

### What Works Well

- Sentences are short and second person; no ALL-CAPS disclaimers, and the warranty and liability sections still say what they need to.
- "You're in control of what you send" restates the product promise (nothing is sent without confirmation) at the point where responsibility is assigned — reassuring rather than defensive.
- The scraping section is candid about the trade-off (no login, no CAPTCHA bypass, polite rate limits, sources can be switched off).

### Priority Recommendations

1. **Stop hyphenating English** in body text.
2. **Two-column contents at tablet widths.**
3. **Fix the footer spacing** (shared with every page).
