## Design Critique: Terms of Use (`/terms/`)

### Overall Impression

The r1 fixes hold: no hyphenated English, a two-column contents list at 768, correct footer text. The page is short enough to read, but the first screen still asks the reader to pick a section before they learn the four things that matter: it's free and as-is, nothing is sent without them, sources may restrict scraping, they can leave any time.

### Usability

| Finding                                                                                                                                                                                        | Severity    | Recommendation                                                                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No at-a-glance summary before the contents; the first "In short" is below the 12-row contents at 375. Same pattern problem as the Privacy Policy, and both legal pages should behave the same. | 🟡 Moderate | Add "The short version" (4 key points) under the effective date, from the same `LegalLayout` mechanism as the Privacy Policy. |
| No governing-law section (carried).                                                                                                                                                            | 🟢 Minor    | Logged in r1; owner decision.                                                                                                 |

### Visual Hierarchy

- **What draws the eye first**: title → effective date → contents card. After the fix: title → short version → contents.
- **Reading flow**: sections are short (2–6 sentences); bullets in "Your account", "You're in control of what you send", "Jobs from other websites" and "Fair use" scan well.
- **Emphasis**: even, as terms should be.

### Consistency

| Element      | Issue                                     | Recommendation                           |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Legal layout | Identical to the Privacy Policy after r1. | Keep the short-version block shared too. |

### Accessibility

- **Color contrast**: unchanged, pass.
- **Touch targets**: contents rows 48 px.
- **Text readability**: 16–17 px body, ≤ 68 ch, no hyphenation; reflows at 320 px.

### What Works Well

- Plain, specific rules ("Don't use BruteForceHR to send unsolicited or bulk messages…") instead of generic prohibitions.
- The liability section protects consumer rights explicitly, which reads as fair rather than defensive.

### Priority Recommendations

1. **Add "The short version"** above the contents.
