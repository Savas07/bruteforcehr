Senior Specialist, CAE Engineering (m/f/d)

Bayreuth, DE

CYBEX is a company that puts tomorrow’s people at the heart of its brand. Always questioning the status quo and not afraid of challenging the establishment, CYBEX has become not just a leader in child safety but is seen as an innovative lifestyle and fashion brand for parents. Your seat to fill:

  • Oversee and coordinate CAE activities for child seats, wheeled goods, furniture, and sports goods developed in Europe, China, and the US
  • Define processes, material models, and best practices for CAE in all phases of product development
  • Collaborate closely with global testing and engineering departments
  • Investigate module-level testing processes and perform static simulations to ensure product integrity and safety
  • Manage CAE activities and communication with external service providers
  • Establish new simulation environments and correlate them with internal and external test results
  • Support in optimizing structural essential geometries and material selection with respect to performance, cost, and weight
  • Provide expertise in fatigue and damage tolerance calculations to support product development and testing
  • Utilize finite element method and multi-body dynamics methodologies along with simulation software tools to conduct analyses and simulations

Your stroll to CYBEX:

  • Completed a degree in Mechanical Engineering or a comparable field of education
  • Professional experience in finite element analysis or multi-body dynamics, and experience in fatigue and damage tolerance calculation
  • Experience in CAE automation and advanced simulation techniques, with exposure to AI-driven tools, will be a strong advantage
  • Knowledge of FEM and MBD and relevant simulation software tools like HyperWorks, LS-PrePost, LS-DYNA, Radioss, HyperLife
  • CAE experience in the field of occupant safety, wheeled goods, or furniture is a plus
  • Independent and structured way of working, as well as analytical thinking and reliability
  • Willingness to support global projects through modified work shift or travel
  • Excellent communication and collaboration skills
  • Fluency in English; German would be a plus
